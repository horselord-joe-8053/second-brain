# Produce Preserver

The Produce Preserver is an important component of the KitchenAid® French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator that helps prolong the freshness of produce by absorbing ethylene gas. Proper installation and maintenance are essential for its effectiveness.

## Maintenance
Regular checks and replacements are necessary to ensure the produce preserver functions effectively.

# Samsung Smart Home

Samsung Smart Home is a mobile application designed to manage and control Samsung smart appliances, including the RF28K9070SG refrigerator. The app allows users to monitor and adjust settings remotely, enhancing the convenience of appliance management.

## Features
- Remote control of refrigerator settings.
- Notifications for maintenance and alerts.
- Integration with other Samsung smart devices.

## Troubleshooting
The app may encounter connectivity issues, which can be resolved by ensuring proper network settings and app updates.

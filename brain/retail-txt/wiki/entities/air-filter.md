# Air Filter

The Air Filter is a critical component of the KitchenAid® French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator, designed to reduce odors inside the refrigerator. It is significantly more effective than baking soda for odor reduction.

## Maintenance
Regular replacement of the air filter is recommended to maintain optimal performance and freshness within the refrigerator.

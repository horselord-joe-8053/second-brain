# Diagnostic Mode

Diagnostic Mode is a feature in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator that allows technicians to check the operational status of various components. This mode is essential for troubleshooting and identifying errors within the appliance.

## Functionality
- Enables testing of sensors and operational components.
- Displays error codes for quick diagnosis.
- Assists in verifying the functionality of the refrigerator's systems.

# Repair Guide for Samsung RF28K9070SG Refrigerator

This repair guide provides detailed troubleshooting steps and diagnostic procedures for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. It is intended for use by technicians with electrical, electronic, and mechanical experience.

## Fast Track Troubleshooting
- A method for quickly diagnosing issues with the RF28K9070SG refrigerator.
- Important for technicians to identify and resolve problems efficiently.

## Diagnostic Mode
- Allows technicians to check the operational status of various components in the refrigerator.
- Essential for troubleshooting and identifying errors.

## Error Codes
- Specific codes that indicate faults or issues within the refrigerator's systems.
- Helps technicians pinpoint problems based on the displayed error codes.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. **Entering Diagnostic Mode**: 
   - Hold Freezer and Flex zone for 6 seconds until the screen goes blank and you hear a beep.
   - Press Flex zone again to enter the first mode.

2. **Exiting Diagnostic Mode**: 
   - After mode 5, the screen will be blank. If left alone for 1 minute, it will exit to normal operation.

3. **Voltage Measurements**: 
   - Ensure circuit ground is used for measuring voltage of any DC component.
   - A sensor resistance over 100K ohm is considered OPEN; under 2K ohm is considered Shorted.

## Symptoms and Actions
- **Condensation on Front Corners**: 
  - This is not a system failure; ensure the door is closed properly.
  
- **Ice Maker Not Making Ice**: 
  - Wait for 12 hours if all ice has been used; check water tank and settings.

- **Water Dispenser Not Functioning**: 
  - Check if the water tank is frozen or improperly installed.

## Samsung Smart Home App
- The app is available for smartphones running Android 4.0 or higher, or iOS 7.0 or higher.
- Ensure the app is connected to the refrigerator and that the router is operating normally.

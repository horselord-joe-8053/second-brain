## Safety, safe work, and disposal — KA-SAFE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 3–4, 7–14, 17–18, 26–27, 31–32]

- Connect only to properly **grounded three-prong electrical supply**; no removal of ground prong, adapter, or extension cord. Disconnect before service and before ice-maker installation on kit-ready models; reinstall protective covers before use. A qualified technician must install the water line and ice maker. **Electrical rating:** **115 V, 60 Hz AC, 15 or 20 A fused supply**; guide recommends a dedicated circuit and outlet not controlled by wall switch (p. 12).
- Use at least **two people** to move/install; refrigerator is heavy. Remove doors from discarded refrigerators but leave shelves in place to deter child entrapment. Supervise children; no flammable gas/solvents/explosive aerosol cans in or near cabinet; use only nonflammable cleaners. Use sturdy glass for ice dispensing to prevent cuts.
- Do not use unauthorized electrical devices inside storage compartments. Use only potable water, with microbiological disinfection caution for water of unknown/unsafe quality. Keep doors/hinges and fingers clear; disconnect power before removing doors. If power cord is damaged, manufacturer or qualified service must replace it.
- Cold tempered glass shelves may shatter if washed with warm water; avoid impacts. Sharp tools can damage ice-bin/chute mechanisms; use **plastic utensil** for ice clumps where the guide directs. Remove external power before cleaning; control Cooling OFF is not a substitute for unplugging before electrical work.
- The manual contains user operating and installation instructions, **not** compressor, sealed-system, or wiring-level service tests. Refer such work to designated qualified service. Preserve all warnings accompanying door removal, water hookup and cleaning.

## Parts and operating characteristics — KA-FEATURE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 5–6]

A high-efficiency variable-speed compressor may operate longer than the owner's previous refrigerator, without constituting a fault. **Dual evaporators on some models** provide separate refrigerator and freezer climates, reducing odor transfer and frost. The full-width temperature-controlled pantry drawer holds large trays; pull-out tray creates a flat storage area. Door ice storage frees interior shelf space. **Water filter** is NSF-certified for stated claims; **air filter** is claimed to reduce common food odors; **produce preserver** absorbs ethylene emitted by fruits and vegetables to slow ripening. LED lighting is not a routine user bulb replacement. A door alarm and power-outage indicator are incorporated in the control interface.

## Refrigeration temperature controls — KA-CTRL-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 21–22]

Factory recommendations: **fridge 37°F (3°C)** and **freezer 0°F (−18°C)**. Wait **24 hours after initial installation before loading food** and at least **24 hours between temperature adjustments**. If the system is too warm/cold, first check for blocked air vents. A lower numeric set point does not make the compartment cool faster. Control screen normally sleeps after **2 minutes** without buttons/paddles; press any button to wake.

**View / change:** Hold **TEMPERATURE 3 seconds** to enter temperature mode. Press TEMPERATURE to switch between refrigerator/freezer set points. Within zone use **LOCK under +** to increase (warmer) or **OPTIONS under −** to decrease (colder). Press **MEASURED FILL (Confirm)** to save; **ICE MODE (Back)** exits without saving, or display returns after ~60 seconds inactivity. In Temperature mode, **LIGHT (Units)** toggles Fahrenheit/Celsius. Refrigerator adjustment range **33–45°F (0–7°C)**; freezer **−5 to 5°F (−21 to −15°C)**. For fridge too cold/warm, adjust warmer/cooler **1° at a time**; similarly freezer. Don't confuse displayed *set point* with a calibrated real-time temperature measurement.

**Turn Cooling OFF/ON:** hold **LOCK + MEASURED FILL together for 3 seconds**. “Refrigeration Cooling is Off” appears while off; repeat to turn cooling back on. Both compartments stop being cooled in OFF state. To avoid unintended lock/menu actions, press the two simultaneously. Before physical repair, disconnect plug regardless of cooling setting.

**Customer aliases:** “temperature screen black,” “set fridge 37 degrees,” “how to switch Celsius,” “why not colder after setting to minimum,” “refrigerator cooling off message.” The control layout illustration is under *Using the Controls*, printed pp. 21–22.

## Extra modes, alarms and display messages — KA-MODES-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 22–23]

- **Max Cool:** press OPTIONS, then **LOCK** (under Max Cool). Helps when large grocery loads, frequent access, or temporarily warm room. Remains active **24 hours** unless turned off by same sequence.
- **Max Ice:** press OPTIONS then **TEMPERATURE** (under Max Ice). Assists periods of increased demand; remains active **24 hours** unless toggled off the same way.
- **Sabbath Mode:** hold **LIGHT + OPTIONS 3 seconds**; temperature set points remain, but interior/dispenser lights, sounds, alarms and dispenser paddles are disabled. Repeat to exit. Max Cool/Max Ice do not automatically resume; Cooling OFF cancels Sabbath. Power-outage warning will not be shown when an outage occurs in Sabbath Mode.
- **Vacation Mode:** hold **TEMPERATURE + OPTIONS 3 seconds**; `VAC` appears. Freezer defrosts less frequently to conserve energy. Repeat to exit. During initial **1 hour**, opening/closing fridge door will not cancel, but afterward door open/close cancels. Activating Max Ice/Max Cool/Sabbath cancels vacation mode.
- **Customer question: “What should I do if my power-outage PO light is showing?”** **Power outage display `PO`:** when supply interrupted and freezer reaches **18°F (−8°C) or above**, `PO` flashes on restoration and other controls/dispensers are disabled until acknowledged with **MEASURED FILL**. This acknowledges display; the guide does not promise that food has remained safe.
- **Door ajar:** if refrigerator door or freezer drawer open **5 minutes** while cooling enabled, alarm sounds and repeats every **2 minutes**; closure cancels. Press any button to temporarily mute while cleaning; warning icon remains. This is different from a power outage alert.
- **Sounds:** hold **ICE MODE + MEASURED FILL 3 seconds** to turn off normal button/dispense sounds (alert tones remain); repeat to restore.
- **Showroom mode:** if display says `SHOWROOM MODE`, hold **LIGHT + LOCK 3 seconds** to exit. Do not confuse with Cooling OFF.
- **Filter Status:** in Options menu filter life percentage is displayed 100–0; `ORDER FILTER` yellow, `REPLACE FILTER` red; correct new cartridge and housing closure automatically reset where described (pp. 17, 23).

## External water dispenser: installation, flushing and measured fill — KA-DISP-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 24–26]

The dispenser will not operate while **either a refrigerator door or freezer drawer is open**. Allow **3 hours** after installation for chilled water. After a water connection or filter change, use a large sturdy container and repeat **5 seconds paddle pressed / 5 seconds released** until water flows; then continue pulses until total **3 US gal (12 L)** have been dispensed. Air can cause intermittent spurting and drips; use this documented purge rather than guessing a valve problem.

**Measured Fill:** because household water pressure affects precision, calibrate using an **accurate 1-cup / 237 mL measuring cup**, flat stable support, and the guide's steps. Press **OPTIONS + LOCK together for 3 seconds** to enter calibration; `1 Cup` / `Back` appear. Press/release paddle to fill to actual one-cup mark; if overshoot, discard and restart via **ICE MODE (Back)**. Confirm using **MEASURED FILL** once exactly filled. The dispenser then uses the calibration for selected quantities; if results become inaccurate, repeat calibration. Refer to complete graphical prompts under *Calibrate Measured Fill*, pp. 24–25. **Measured Fill** may time out or require reactivation between volumes; its screen options and selectable volumes are explained at p. 25. Do not claim milliliter-level guaranteed accuracy or use kitchen substitutes that are not calibrated.

**Ice:** select **ICE MODE** cubed or crushed; hold a sturdy glass near outlet, push paddle; release to stop. Dispense may continue making noise or dropping ice briefly after release; switching from crushed to cubed may release crushed pieces first. Ice particles/clumps can block chute: regularly empty/clean chute, bin and underneath, about every **2 weeks** if dispensing is infrequent; use **plastic utensil** for obstructions, a warm damp cloth, and dry fully. **Dispenser drip-tray insert** lifts out by pressing back edge and emptying in sink. **Light** cycles **OFF → ON (100% brightness) → DIM (50%)**. **Lock** hold **3 seconds** disables dispenser paddles/controls for cleaning or avoiding unintended use; does not disconnect refrigerator or turn off icemaker (p. 26).

**Customer aliases:** “fill one cup inaccurate,” “paddle runs after release,” “water after new filter,” “why no water when door open,” “ice clogs chute.”

## Icemaker and bucket variants — KA-ICE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 14, 26–27]

After first hookup allow **24 hours** for first batch; discard **first three batches**; bin takes **2–3 days** to fill fully. Normal rate approximately **3 lb (1.4 kg) per day**; use **Max Ice** mode to boost temporary production. Do not keep food/objects in ice bucket. Avoid water supply treated with softener chemicals where possible; if unavoidable, ensure softener properly maintained. Don't break clumped ice with a metal tool.

**Refrigerator-compartment icemaker (some models):** high on left side, storage bin on left refrigerator door. Open left ice compartment door via latch; on/off switch **left = ON**, **right = OFF**. Bin release hole/latch at its base; lift bin and pull straight out, reinsert and press firmly downward. Auto sensor pauses ice if bin removed, door open, or bin full, even though switch remains ON.

**Freezer-compartment icemaker (other models):** wire shutoff arm **down = ON**, **up until click = OFF**. Full bucket automatically raises arm; **do not force** it up or down. Turn maker off before removing bucket to serve/clean, switch on after replacing bin.

These **two icemaker controls must not be combined or guessed from another variant**. For visual confirmation direct customer to *Ice Maker and Ice Storage Bin (on some models)*, printed p. 27, unlabeled switch/bin illustrations.

## Water supply installation and operating limits — KA-PLUMB-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 12–14, 40]

Household potable cold supply should deliver **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)** to dispenser/ice system (filter performance sheet p. 40 confirms). Reverse osmosis installation has separate supply requirements: **40–60 psi (276–414 kPa)** to RO equipment; consult p. 12 and qualified plumber if uncertain. The source instructs a **qualified technician** for water line/ice-maker installation (p. 3). It describes 1/2 in (12.7 mm) household branch, 1/4 in (6.35 mm) outside-diameter soft copper or specified PEX supply, compression sleeves/nuts, flush before final connection, supply shutoff and leak-check. Do not use a piercing-type or 3/16 in saddle valve; avoid kinking and freezing. Example recommended PEX kits: W10505928RP (7 ft), 8212547RP (5 ft), W10267701RP (25 ft). The refrigerator inlet may be one of **Style 1 or Style 2** fittings; match the actual coupling rather than using one undifferentiated procedure.

**Documented commissioning:** unplug and shut off main water, attach suitable shutoff valve to cold line, route copper/PEX, flush until clear, attach to appropriate refrigerator inlet (hand/thread/compression directions depend on style), make service loop, fasten clamp to reduce coupling strain, open shutoff and tighten leaks without overtightening. Plug into grounded outlet and flush system as in `KA-DISP-001`. Qualified plumbing must comply with codes. Visual references: *Connect to Water Line* and *Connect to Refrigerator*, printed pp. 12–14, with labeled tubing/coupling diagrams.

## Water filter replacement, status and product identification — KA-WFILTER-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 17–18, 20, 23, 40]

**Location:** filter housing inside the **right side of refrigerator ceiling**. Open filter door by lifting release; align filter-label arrow with notch, insert and turn **clockwise 90°/¼ turn** to lock; lift filter into compartment and close housing door. A filter that is not locked correctly can leak or prevent water dispensing. Follow **3-gal (12 L)** post-filter flush `KA-DISP-001`. Removal: open door and rotate filter **counterclockwise** then pull straight out; wipe drips. **Replace at least every 6 months**, earlier if flow decreases. Filter display `ORDER` yellow and `REPLACE` red; proper replacement/housing closure generally automatically resets status, and missing/wrongly installed filter leaves warning. Do not invent a manual reset key sequence.

**Part distinctions from THIS document:** accessories p. 20 gives water filter **W10413645A / FILTER2**; performance sheet p. 40 identifies **P9WB2L/P9RFWB2L** system, replacement **P9RFWB2L / EDR2RXD1 / EDR2RXD1B**. Confirm the physical cartridge and manufacturer's stated ordering designation before ordering; the document contains more than one part notation. Performance sheet rated capacity **200 gal (757 L)**, nominal flow **0.75 gpm (2.84 L/min)** at 60 psi, operating potable well/city water **35–120 psi**, temperature **33–100°F (1–38°C)**. The sheet states NSF/ANSI 42, 53 and 401 performance categories with contaminant-specific tested claims; consult **p. 40, PERFORMANCE DATA SHEET** for a particular substance and reduction numbers, rather than generalizing certified filtration to every contaminant.

**Limits:** never use microbiologically unsafe or unknown-quality water without appropriate disinfection as described. Filter testing under laboratory conditions does not guarantee user's local contaminant profile. Refer other service questions to qualified technicians.

## Air filter and produce preserver — KA-AIR-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 18–20]

**Air filter (when fitted):** mounted behind vented door on rear refrigerator wall. Install supplied filter, open door, snap in place; activate status indicator by pressing bubble on back until it pops. Depending on backplate notches, indicator may mount behind the door or in an alternate visible position. Replace air filter **every six months**, using side tabs to remove. Appearance/status of activated indicator helps indicate replacement; keep it accessible.

**Produce Preserver (when fitted):** a pair of replaceable pouches in perforated housing to absorb ethylene and delay some produce ripening. Install according to enclosed setup including indicator activation and mounting; replace pouches **every six months** or when indicator says, and keep housing clear. Manufacturer provides a caution about preservative contents and cleaning skin/eye exposure (p. 19); do not recommend opening pouches or sprinkling contents on food. Refer to *Installing the Produce Preserver* and *Installing the Status Indicator*, pp. 19–20, for exact mounting variants. Do not claim all foods benefit equally.

## Storage, cabinet parts and adjustment — KA-STORAGE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 5, 15–16, 21, 28–30]

**Doors:** French-door left leaf carries automatically folding hinged vertical seal; it folds inward while open and seals the meeting line when both leaves close (p. 21). Door misalignment can disrupt operation. **Crispers** have automatically regulated humidity; remove covers/pans with correct stops, clips and shelf positions. **Pantry drawer**, pull-out tray, freezer drawer, shelf supports, bins, and adjustable dividers have distinct removal procedures with release points; do not lever blindly. Refer to *REFRIGERATOR FEATURES*, pp. 28–30 for unlabeled mechanism drawings. Avoid hot-water washing of cold glass. Meat-storage instructions on p. 30 distinguish packaging and times by meat type; the customer's food should be handled within those stated limits, not general improvised advice.

**Handles and leveling:** p. 14–16 specifies two refrigerator handles, freezer handle(s), supplied **1/8 in hex key**, setscrews and correct orientation (drawer handles are more curved). Tighten partial setscrew pairs as directed and check flush fit. Feet/leveling mechanisms determine refrigerator tilt and door alignment; ensure doors even at top and closure works. Consult illustrations under *Handle Installation and Removal*, p. 14–15 and *Refrigerator Leveling, Door Closing and Alignment*, p. 16.

## Door removal and reinstall — KA-DOOR-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 8–11]

Before moving the unit through a passage, **measure opening**; remove doors only when necessary. Turn Cooling OFF, disconnect power, clear door bins and contents, use the printed tools **3/16 in hex-key wrench and #2 Phillips screwdriver**. Right- and left-door hinge removal differ: left carries water tubing and wiring; disconnect the applicable style coupling before lifting and keep door supported. The guide illustrates water tube **Style 1 / Style 2** connectors, top right/left hinge wiring, hinge covers and fasteners. Reinstall with all electrical/water connections secure and check doors. **Freezer drawer front** removal has its own rail/fastener method; reattach with designated screws and ensure even seated position. These are detailed mechanical steps with heavy-door and live-wire hazards: advise installer to follow the *Remove and Replace Refrigerator Doors*, pp. 8–10, and *Remove and Replace Freezer Drawer Front*, p. 11, verbatim step order and printed connection illustrations. This handbook does not improvise shortcuts.

## Cleaning, condenser, absence and lighting — KA-CARE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 31–32]

Disconnect power before interior care. Wash removable parts with compatible mild/nonflammable cleaner; use soft cloth, gentle surfaces and thoroughly dry. Stainless steel has explicit cleaning restrictions: improper chemicals can permanently damage finish; read *Cleaning*, p. 31 before using a commercial agent. LED lighting is not intended for user bulb replacement; service if failed. **Condenser cleaning** generally isn't needed in normal home conditions; if very greasy/dusty/pet-hair environment, p. 32 describes removing base grille, soft-brush vacuum and reassembling safely. **Vacation/moving** instructions on p. 32 distinguish leaving unit running, turning it off, emptying food and disconnecting water/ice, packing and moving safely. Do not infer from the shorter control Vacation Mode that long-term food safety is assured.

## Warranty and support — KA-WARRANTY-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 1, 20, 41, 43]

**Customer question aliases:** “How long is the sealed refrigeration system warranty?”, “Does year seven cover parts and labor?”, “What is the KitchenAid compressor warranty?” KitchenAid publishes a **TEN YEAR LIMITED WARRANTY** with tiered coverage, valid per stated US/Canada conditions when appliance installed/used/maintained as instructed. **Year 1:** parts and labor for defects as stated. **Years 2–5:** cavity liner cracking due to defects, and sealed refrigeration system (compressor, evaporator, condenser, dryer, connecting tubing), **parts and labor**. **Years 6–10:** specified sealed refrigeration components **parts only, labor excluded**. This is **not** unconditional ten-year full coverage. Original purchase proof, model/serial, description, contact details required; factory-specified parts/authorized service. Exclusions include commercial/nonresidential use, teaching visits, improper installation/house wiring/plumbing, consumables (filter/preservation media), non-genuine accessory damage, accidents/acts of God, unauthorized repairs, finishes/cosmetics outside narrow provisions, corrosive environments, food loss, pickup, remote-travel costs, inaccessible appliances and altered serial numbers. Check exact jurisdiction and purchase date before promise.

For US support the guide prints **1-800-422-1230**, Canada **1-800-807-6777**; replacement accessories US **1-800-901-2042** (p. 20). `kitchenaid.com`, `kitchenaid.ca` (printed p. 1/43). Manufacturer limited warranty dated **04/17** in this guide. Do not assume contact routes remain currently active; only source facts reported.

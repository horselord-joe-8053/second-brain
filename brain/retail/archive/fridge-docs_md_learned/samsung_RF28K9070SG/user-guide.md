# Technician knowledge handover — Samsung four-door refrigerator: user manual

**Only technical authority:** *Refrigerator — User manual — Free Standing Appliance* (Samsung; English printed pages 1–76; edition whose English pages carry print date 2018-07-05). **Archive location:** `samsung_RF28K9070SG/user-guide.pdf`. This is an independently authored, text-only knowledge handbook based on **this user manual alone**, not the separately supplied repair guide. The English manual discusses a family of variants; `RF28K9070SG` is a folder identifier and does not by itself establish every optional part or SmartThings setup. **Evidence:** `[DOCUMENTED]` or `[UNKNOWN]` only.

## Answering policy and scope — SAM-ROUTE-001

For each ticket, establish the **exact full model**, which compartment is involved (**Fridge, Freezer, or FlexZone**), whether the control panel is locked, and what the customer actually observes. Retrieve matching `[DOCUMENTED]` customer instructions before applying a targeted `[UNKNOWN]` guard; use both for partially answerable questions. A missing troubleshooting entry does not justify guessing a broken part. The source supports user operation, settings, cleaning, installation and specified checks, **not arbitrary component-level electrical tests**. Safety warnings govern every instruction. The customer has the same edition of this PDF; give them the literal printed page/heading for a useful drawing, rather than attaching or reconstructing a picture. No images, HTML or external assets are part of this Markdown.

## Refrigerator parts and compartments — SAM-PARTS-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 16–18]

The installation illustration identifies the **Fridge** upper compartment, **Freezer** lower compartment and separate **FlexZone** lower-right space (FlexZone setting explained p. 39). Labeled components include auto icemaker, wine rack on applicable models, EZ Fresh vegetable/fruit drawers, shelf types, freezer door bins, water filter and other optional accessories. The manual's numbered view is under *Installation — Refrigerator at a glance*, printed p. 17. It warns that the hinged vertical door section should fold into the correct position when closing; forcing a door can pop the opposite door. Opening a door disables the icemaker auger for safety. If interior/exterior LED fails, contact Samsung service rather than fitting a substitute (p. 17).

## Personal safety, appliance handling and disposal — SAM-SAFE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 4–15, 19–35, 59–62]

**Electricity:** do not use a wet hand on plug, unsafe/damaged cord, overloaded/loose outlet, improper grounding, or inappropriate extension. Avoid placing water-filled containers on appliance and water near exposed electrical parts. Disconnect power before specified cleaning/door-removal tasks, and seek qualified service when plug/cord or internal equipment is damaged. Children must not hang on doors, enter compartments, pull drawers for climbing, or touch hinges; pinch, suffocation, falling and frostbite hazards. Keep flammable gases, solvents, volatile chemicals and aerosol cans away. Do not introduce unauthorized electrical appliances into refrigerator. Do not touch frozen food/surfaces with wet hands.

**Foods and surfaces:** no completely thawed refreezing per manual; avoid fizzy drinks in freezer and food packages positioned unsafely. Do not turn tempered glass shelf upside down or wash cold glass with warm water: shelves may shatter. Do not keep vegetable oils in door bins (p. 12). Keep fingers/utensils away from ice delivery chute, bucket ejector and electrical assemblies; never substitute a third-party bucket. Wipe ice/water spilled on floor to avoid slipping. Do not attempt to replace internal LEDs yourself: electric shock risk (p. 62).

**Cleaning:** no benzene, thinner, household/car detergents or abrasive/corrosive products; microfiber/soft dry/damp cloth as directed, unplug and dry connectors/plug contact points, p. 59. **Refrigerant/environment:** follow installation and disposal warnings pp. 4–15; do not pierce cooling lines or perform DIY repairs. Remove doors from a discarded refrigerator and protect children against entrapment. **Regulatory:** FCC notice and Canadian IC wireless module information are on pp. 14–15; modifications can void wireless operation authorization. California Proposition 65 statement is on p. 5. Do not confuse regulatory communication IDs with diagnostic error codes.

## Installation requirements, clearance and dimensional lookup — SAM-INSTALL-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 16, 19–35]

**Location:** choose firm, level floor strong enough for fully loaded appliance and ventilation; ambient range **50–110°F (10–43°C)**. Provide opening clearance and avoid heat/sun/wet environments. Page 27 tabulates dimensions: **Depth A 34¼ in (871 mm)**, **Width B 35¾ in (908 mm)**, **Height C 70¾ in (1797 mm)**, **Overall Height D 71⅞ in (1825 mm)**. The plan-view swing/clearance drawing on p. 28 gives more required measurements: **2 in (50 mm)** in the shown location, **125° door angle**, 59⅝ in (1515 mm), 11⅞ in (303 mm), 12 in (305 mm), 35¾ in (908 mm), 29⅜ in (748 mm), 2¾ in (70 mm), 1¾ in (43 mm), 49 in (1245 mm), indexed to dimensions **01–10**. Refer to *Installation — STEP 1 Select a site*, printed pp. 27–28 and its **numbered door-swing illustration** before asserting where each number applies; distances are not interchangeable.

**Floor, leveling and gaps:** pages 29–32 require stable floor and adjustments to left/right levelling feet. Door height can be corrected with provided **snap rings** in hinge gap; do not raise door excessively or contact upper cover. On applicable **RF28K907*** variants the top right **gap bolt** adjusts gap between doors (p. 32); don't apply gap-bolt method to other models without confirmation. For physical door-gap adjustment follow the numbered illustration beneath *STEP 4 Adjust the door height as well as the door gap*, pp. 31–32.

**Moving through entrance / removing doors:** measure passage first. Page 19 starts fridge-door removal; disconnect relevant power, water line and **two wire connectors** at left door, lift hinge with door supported, with some connector locations varying by model (pp. 19–21). Freezer door removal follows **after** refrigerator doors have been removed, per caution (p. 22), using rail/hinge instructions pp. 22–23. Rehang lower/freezer doors before upper refrigerator doors as dictated by the corresponding drawings, pp. 24–26. Restore wiring and water line; do not trap/strain conductors. These are complex, heavy door procedures—refer to the actual step illustrations and use qualified assistance rather than guessing unlisted fasteners.

## Water-line installation and first commissioning — SAM-WATER-INSTALL-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 33–35]

Icemaker operation requires supply pressure **20–125 psi** (p. 33). A low-pressure installation may require corrective measures described by guide or plumbing professional. Water must be potable; fit supplied/compatible cold-water tubing and shutoff valve according to diagram; turn off supply for cutting/connection, use compression nuts without overtightening, and check for leaks. If plastic tubing is used, do not use it without molded bulb/end where specified (p. 35). For exact nipple/tube layout see *STEP 5 Connect the water dispenser line* (pp. 33–35).

**First flush:** the installation section instructs dispensing **1 US gallon (~6 minutes)** before drinking or using dispenser water (p. 35). Give ice maker **1–2 days** and discard first **one or two buckets** of produced ice for the initial water line flush. For startup, plug in, check light, temporarily set coldest and allow about an hour for initial cooling/motor behavior, then await selected temperatures (p. 35). These **initial hookup** instructions differ from the **~7-minute purge after a replacement filter** on p. 61. Do not merge them or substitute values from another brand.

## Feature-panel mapping and temperature controls — SAM-CTRL-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 36–41]

Panel items are printed: **01 Freezer / Power Freeze (3 sec); 02 Fridge / Power Cool (3 sec); 03 Door Alarm; 04 FlexZone; 05 Lighting / Control Lock (3 sec); 06 Ice Maker Off; 07 Cubed Ice / Override; 08 Crushed Ice / Filter Reset; 09 network connection**. When setpoint changes, display may show **actual compartment temperature until it matches selected value**, then setpoint; this is not evidence of nonresponse, p. 36.

**Freezer:** press Freezer repeatedly for **5 to −8°F (−15 to −23°C)** selection. Hold Freezer **3 seconds** to turn **Power Freeze** on or off. Power Freeze runs more aggressively for several hours, then returns to prior setting; for large food additions the manual says activate **at least 20 hours ahead**. Uses more energy (p. 37).

**Fridge:** repeatedly press Fridge to select **34–44°F (1–7°C)**. Hold **Fridge 3 seconds** to toggle **Power Cool**, which speeds cooling for several hours, then returns to previous setpoint. Manual suggests quickly cooling perishable food or after door was left open; it consumes more electricity (pp. 37–38).

**Door alarm and units:** press **Door Alarm** to enable/disable. Factory enabled; sounds and indicator blinks if door open more than **2 minutes**. Hold Door Alarm **3 seconds** to toggle °C / °F (p. 38).

**Dispenser lighting:** pressing Lighting selects continuous light or returns to default (normally lamp only while dispensing). **Control Lock:** hold **Lighting / Control Lock 3 seconds** to block controls and both ice/water delivery; hold again to unlock. **Device authentication:** when prompted by compatible SmartThings/smart appliance workflow, hold Control Lock **5 seconds**, display shows `on` about 5 seconds; separate from 3-second local lock toggle (p. 40).

**Ice maker OFF:** hold **Ice Maker Off 3 seconds** to disable production even if Cubed/Crushed selected; repeat to resume. Cubed Ice and Crushed Ice buttons choose dispensed type. **Cubed Ice held 3 seconds** also toggles Smart Grid **Override**; do not confuse this with choosing cubic ice (p. 40, p. 47).

**Crushed Ice held 3 seconds** resets **filter lifecycle** after replacing cartridge; reminder usually blinks red after approximately **six months or 320 US gallons (1200 L)**, but water chemistry/flow can require earlier replacement (p. 41). **Cooling Off / Shop mode:** hold **Fridge + Freezer + Door Alarm together 5 seconds** to toggle; `OFF` display and chime means compressor cooling is disabled, while lights/fans can still operate (p. 41). This setting is not safe electrical isolation for repair.

**Customer aliases:** “OFF on temperature panel,” “how to convert C/F,” “Power Freeze button,” “red filter indicator,” “ice switch is off,” “panel locked, water not dispensing.” Confirm exact button from the *Feature panel*, pp. 36–41.

## FlexZone four selectable climates — SAM-FLEX-001 [DOCUMENTED; p. 39]

**Customer question: “How do I set the FlexZone Soft Freezing temperature?”** The four mode targets are presets, rather than individual setpoints: Freezer follows freezer; Soft Freezing is 23°F; Chill/Meat is 30°F; Cool is 41°F. FlexZone is lower right; hold **FlexZone 3 seconds**, then press repeatedly to cycle, release when desired:

| Mode | Documented target | Guide's stated uses |
|---|---|---|
| Freezer | Same selected temperature as Freezer compartment | Frozen foods |
| Soft Freezing | **23°F (−5°C)** | Meat/fish for extended storage per guide |
| Chill / Meat | **30°F (−1°C)** | Steak/deli cuts, fish, bacon, cheese |
| Cool | **41°F (5°C)** | Beverages, water/juice/soft drinks, short-term use |

**Warning:** Do not keep meat/food requiring low-temperature storage in **Cool** setting; food may spoil. When switching modes, liquid/moisture may release from meat or other items. If button fails, first unlock using **Lighting/Control Lock** held over 3 seconds until lock icon off. The source does not establish safe holding times for every food category; avoid applying these modes as universal food-safety prescriptions.

## SmartThings app and network — SAM-SMART-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 42–44, 66]

The guide describes finding/installing **SmartThings** through supported mobile app stores, signing into/registering a **Samsung account**, and registering connected refrigerator via the application; network authentication may use **Control Lock for 5 seconds** (p. 40). Feature list includes remote monitoring/control and interactions with registered supported devices; availability depends on app/device/platform and connection. The manual's contemporaneous Android/iOS requirements and user-interface instructions are historical to its edition; do not promise present-day compatibility. If app cannot locate refrigerator: check Wi-Fi/router status, Samsung account login, supported phone, correct device registration, network setup and environmental signal issues (pp. 42–44, 66). Restart app/router or refrigerator only as described in troubleshooting. Do not invent modern app menu screens or cloud API credentials.

## Smart Grid / Demand Response — SAM-GRID-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 45–49, 67]

**Smart Grid is a utility-dependent energy-management feature**, not a refrigerator fault code. It responds to signals from an enrolled/compatible energy provider by reducing load. The manual describes **Delay Appliance Load (DAL)** and **Temporary Appliance Load Reduction (TALR)**, different degrees/durations of operation with indicators. It also describes software-based Energy Management for energy reporting/control through supported network connections (pp. 45–48). To refuse utility demand-response commands temporarily, hold **Cubed Ice/Override 3 seconds**: `Or` / `on` appears and buzzer; repeat to end, `Or` / `oF` appears (p. 47). SmartThings app Energy Management can also control override. Smart Grid operation requires compatible **Energy Management System (EMS)**, utility registration and working Internet; use p. 67 troubleshooting before attributing missing Smart Grid function to hardware. Open-standard interoperability and license notices are on pp. 49, 73. Avoid asserting that participation is available in all utility districts.

## Water and ice dispensing, clouding and storage — SAM-DISP-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 50–55]

Select desired ice type with Cubed/Crushed Ice and push a suitable glass against dispenser lever for water/ice. Door open or locked panel can disable output. Ice maker and bucket make cubes automatically; if production stops, check Ice Maker Off, bucket seat, door closure and supply. **Water clouding** after dispensing is discussed on p. 53: trapped dissolved gases/pressure change may produce transient cloudy appearance; do not equate every discoloration, odor or unsafe supply with this normal explanation. The manual discusses a dispenser/water filter and core alkaline water-flow explanation for the temporary appearance; only customer-supplied water tests determine other contamination.

**Ice bucket:** after long inactivity ice may clump; remove carefully, discard clumps and replace bin firmly. The bucket is not a food-storage container; do not force door, use sharp tools or put hands in ice chute. A blinking **Ice Off** indicator may mean bucket must be removed/reseated. With door open neither icemaker nor dispenser operates (p. 55). Icemaker can make operational noises. **Wine rack** and shelf placements (p. 54) vary by model.

## Water-filter removal / installation and RO limits — SAM-FILTER-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 59–62]

Samsung says use **Samsung-provided or approved filter**; unapproved cartridge may leak and damage appliance (p. 59). Filter indicator turns red at replacement time. **Shut water line off before removal.** Open filter cover, turn cartridge knob **counterclockwise 90°/¼ turn** to unlock, pull out (expect possible dripping). Insert the new approved filter and turn **clockwise to lock**; confirm it seats in correct housing position. **Hold Crushed Ice 3 seconds** for filter-life reset; then turn water supply back on and run dispenser water for approximately **7 minutes** to purge air/impurities. Some initial spurting and drips may be normal after exchange; dry any spill (pp. 60–61). Refer to unlabeled step illustrations under *Maintenance — Water filter*, pp. 59–61; never guess part compatibility based on shape.

**Reverse osmosis:** supply pressure to RO system **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)**, RO-to-fridge cold line **at least 40 psi (276 kPa)**. If low, check RO sediment filter and tank, and consider the additional pressure drop caused by a refrigerator filter, as specified (p. 62). Plumbing professional should handle service. New filter orders: `samsungparts.com` or local Samsung service center. LEDs require Samsung service, not DIY (p. 62).

## Shelves, drawers and cleaning — SAM-CARE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 54–59]

The guide explains how to lift/remove/reposition glass refrigerator shelves, door bins, EZ Fresh drawers and foldable vegetable shelf; use each rail/stop as illustrated. Door bin top section has a marked removal line; lift and draw out rather than prying. Wine rack can mount to specified sliding shelf in FlexZone area (p. 54). For physical removal under *Maintenance — Handle and care*, pp. 55–58, refer to the actual unlabeled pictorial steps; this text guide does not invent the direction of hidden clips. Before cleaning, unplug as directed; wipe mild-compatible materials only and avoid prohibited chemicals. For water filter, isolate water source first (p. 59). When ice bin is removed/installed, ensure proper seating and dry meltwater to avoid slips.

## Customer troubleshooting — SAM-TROUBLE-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 63–67]

| Complaint / search synonym | Documented checks and limited support advice |
|---|---|
| Refrigerator won't run / won't cool | Check plugged in securely, correct temperature setpoints, away from heat or poor ventilation, door fully closed, and not in Cooling Off (Shop) mode; p. 63. |
| Food in fridge freezing | Control setpoint too cold, low ambient temperature and food directly at cooling vents/coldest area can matter; adjust as guide specifies; p. 63. |
| Clicking, buzzing or crackling sounds | Some cooling/defrost/ice sounds are normal; consult p. 63 symptoms; if severe/unusual don't claim motor failure from sound alone. |
| Cabinet front/sides warm | Anti-condensation heat-proof piping may warm adjacent surfaces; normal when conditions match p. 64. |
| Exterior moisture or internal condensation | Humidity and door open/door seal matter; confirm proper closure and environment (pp. 64–65). |
| No ice / icemaker stopped | If bucket depleted allow up to **12 hours** for fresh production; check **Ice Maker Off** indicator and hold 3 seconds to toggle, freezer temperature below **32°F (0°C)** or **23°F (−5°C)** in warm ambient conditions per table; clear ice clumps, check water filter and door closure (p. 64). |
| Weak or no water dispensing | Check door open, lock, filter installation/replacement, supply and water pressure; use model-specific documented procedure, not unsupplied solenoid testing (pp. 50, 59–65). |
| Ice clumped/stuck, Ice Off icon flashing | Check/reseat supplied bucket and remove ice clumps, never fingers/sharp objects (p. 55). |
| App says device not found / fails registration | Samsung account, compatible app/phone, Wi-Fi/Internet/router, registration procedure and restarting app/router as printed on p. 66. |
| Smart Grid status absent | Provider/EMS enrollment, Internet and compatibility as p. 67; absence does not prove cooling fault. |
| Water cloudy after dispensing | Guide describes temporary clouding due to filtration/pressure; check whether clears as described and do not promise safety of visibly contaminated or foul-smelling supply (p. 53). |
| Cooling seems stopped but fan/light work | Check if `OFF` Cooling Off shop mode has been enabled via combo **Fridge+Freezer+Door Alarm** (p. 41). |
| FlexZone spoiling stored foods | Verify exact FlexZone mode: **Cool 41°F (5°C)** is for beverages, not meat; p. 39. |

These are source-listed first steps, not guaranteed root causes. If failure persists, escalate through manufacturer service rather than inventing part diagnoses.

## Warranty: never merge U.S. and Canada — SAM-WARRANTY-001 [DOCUMENTED; pp. 68–72]

**Customer question aliases:** “What is the difference between U.S. and Canadian warranty coverage?”, “How long does the inverter compressor warranty last in Canada versus the US?”, “Is the compressor parts-only in Canada?” **United States original consumer purchaser with proof of purchase (p. 68):** **one year parts and labor**; **five years parts and labor on sealed refrigeration system only** (compressor, evaporator, condenser, drier, tubing); **ten years parts and five years labor on Digital Inverter Compressor only**. Warranty covers stated manufacturing defects subject to original purchase, domestic use, manufacturer instructions and authorized Samsung service. Samsung decides repair/replacement/refund under terms, replacement-parts residual guarantee and exclusions; service access, geography and purchase receipt affect eligibility. Pages 69–70 describe limitations such as abuse, faulty utilities/installation, accident, unauthorized repairs, food damage and consequential damages. Do not promise automatic no-cost replacement based solely on age.

**Canada (pp. 71–72):** explicitly different tier: **one year in-home labor, one year parts, ten years inverter compressor part only** under Canadian terms. Samsung Electronics Canada authorized service, original dated sales receipt and geographic availability are required. Exclusions include accident/fire/flood, line voltage, misuse, installation, unauthorized repairs, commercial use and missing/altered serial numbers. Canadian service **1-800-SAMSUNG (726-7864)**, `samsung.com/ca/support` (as printed). Verify jurisdiction **before** selecting a warranty claim.

**Support:** English p. 76 points to `samsung.com/spsn` QR/videos, and Samsung support channels are referenced in warranty; URLs/phone numbers are historical printed references, not live-checked.

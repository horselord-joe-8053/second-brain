# Water Filtration System
The Water Filtration System is an essential component of the KitchenAid® French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator, designed to maintain water quality for drinking and ice-making. It includes installation and maintenance instructions to ensure optimal performance.

## Key Features
- **Model P9WB2L/P9RFWB2L**: Specific model providing critical information on filter replacement and water quality management.
- **Maintenance Procedures**: Detailed steps for installing and replacing filters, as well as information on status indicators.

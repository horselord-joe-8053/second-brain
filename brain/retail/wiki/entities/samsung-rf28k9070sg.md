# Samsung RF28K9070SG  
The Samsung RF28K9070SG is a model of refrigerator manufactured by Samsung Electronics. It is designed with advanced features and smart home integration capabilities, allowing users to connect and control their refrigerator through the Samsung Smart Home application. 

## Key Features  
- Smart Grid functionality for energy management.  
- Water supply connection for ice maker and water dispenser.  
- Adjustable door heights and gap correction features.

Samsung Smart Home is an application that connects to Samsung smart appliances, including the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. This app allows users to integrate their refrigerator with smart home technology, providing enhanced control and monitoring capabilities.

The application is designed for smartphones running Android 4.0 (ICS) or higher, or iOS 7.0 or higher, and is essential for users looking to maximize the functionality of their Samsung appliances.

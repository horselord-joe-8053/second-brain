## Claims
- The document emphasizes the importance of proper cleaning methods to maintain the refrigerator's functionality and appearance.
- Proper installation of the water line is crucial for the refrigerator's operation and must comply with specific plumbing codes.
- Following safety instructions is crucial to prevent injuries and property damage.
- A newly installed refrigerator will take 12 to 24 hours to start producing ice.
- The ice maker produces approximately 100 to 130 ice cubes in a 24-hour period, depending on various conditions.
- Proper installation of the refrigerator door and handle is crucial for functionality and safety.
- The water supply must be connected correctly to avoid leaks and ensure the ice maker operates effectively.
- The warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship for thirty days from the date of purchase.
- The warranty does not cover damages due to misuse, improper installation, or labor costs for diagnosis.
- Many operational sounds are normal for modern refrigerators and do not indicate malfunction.

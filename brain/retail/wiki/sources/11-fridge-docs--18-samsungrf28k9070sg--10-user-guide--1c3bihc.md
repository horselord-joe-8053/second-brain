# Samsung RF28K9070SG User Guide

This document serves as the user manual for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, providing essential safety information, installation guidelines, operational instructions, and warranty details to ensure safe and efficient use of the appliance.

## Safety Information
- The manual emphasizes the importance of safety precautions and proper installation to prevent accidents and ensure efficient operation.
- Extensive safety warnings are provided throughout the manual.

## Installation Instructions
- Detailed steps for connecting the water supply and setting up the refrigerator are included.
- Proper handling and installation are emphasized to avoid hazards such as electric shock, fire, and injury.

### Installation Steps
1. Connect the water supply line to the refrigerator.
2. Plug the power cord into the wall socket.
3. Set the temperature to the coldest setting and wait for about an hour.
4. Confirm the refrigerator is functioning properly.

## Operational Features
- The manual includes descriptions of the feature panel, ice maker, water dispenser, and SmartThings integration.
- Troubleshooting guidance is provided for common issues.

### Troubleshooting Table
| Symptom                                      | Action                                                                                       |
|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The refrigerator does not operate or cool.  | Ensure power cord is connected, temperature control is set correctly, and proper ventilation. |
| Food in the fridge freezes.                  | Adjust temperature settings and check placement of food.                                    |
| The ice maker does not make ice.             | Check ice maker settings, water line connection, and temperature.                           |
| The water dispenser is not functioning.       | Verify water line connection and filter installation.                                        |

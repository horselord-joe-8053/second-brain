# KitchenAid KRFF507HPS User Guide

The KitchenAid KRFF507HPS User Guide provides essential information for the operation and maintenance of the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator. This guide includes safety instructions, installation guidelines, maintenance procedures, and troubleshooting tips.

## Overview
- **Model**: KitchenAid KRFF507HPS
- **Type**: French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator

## Safety Instructions
- Follow all safety precautions to prevent hazards during installation and operation.

## Installation Guidelines
1. Unpack the refrigerator carefully.
2. Connect the refrigerator to the electrical and water supply as per the instructions provided.

## Maintenance Instructions
- **Condenser Cleaning**: Clean the condenser every 2 to 3 months, especially in dusty or greasy environments.
- **Light Operation**: LED lights do not require replacement.
- **Vacation and Moving Care**: Follow specific instructions for managing the refrigerator during vacations and when moving.

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Common issues include the refrigerator not operating, temperature inconsistencies, and ice maker problems. Detailed troubleshooting steps are provided in the guide.

## Water Filtration System
- Ensure the water filter is installed correctly for the water dispenser to operate.

## Air Filter
- The air filter is more effective than baking soda at reducing food odors.

## Produce Preserver
- Instructions for installation and operation of the Produce Preserver are included.

## Diagnostic Mode
To enter Diagnostic Mode:
1. Hold the Freezer and Flex Zone buttons for 6 seconds until the screen goes blank and you hear a beep.
2. Press the Flex Zone button again to enter the first mode.
3. To exit, after mode 5, the screen will be blank. If left alone for 1 minute, it will exit to normal operation.

## Fast Track Troubleshooting
This section provides a quick reference guide for technicians to diagnose and resolve issues with the refrigerator. Common symptoms and actions include:

- **Condensation on the exterior:** Ensure the door is closed properly and check for high ambient temperatures.
- **Ice maker not producing ice:** Wait for 12 hours if the ice bucket is empty, check the Ice Maker Off icon, and ensure the freezer temperature is set correctly.

## Samsung Smart Home App
The Samsung Smart Home app is used to connect and manage smart features of the refrigerator. Troubleshooting steps for the app include:

- Ensure the app is compatible with your smartphone.
- Log into your Samsung account to use the app.
- Check the router's operation if the app fails to connect.

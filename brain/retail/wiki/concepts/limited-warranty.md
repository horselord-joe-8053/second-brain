# Limited Warranty

The limited warranty outlines the coverage period, conditions for warranty service, exclusions, and customer responsibilities for appliances like the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. It specifies the duration for parts and labor, as well as conditions for in-home service, ensuring that customers are informed about their rights and obligations regarding warranty claims.

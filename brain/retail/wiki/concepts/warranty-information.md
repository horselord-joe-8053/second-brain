# Warranty Information
The warranty information for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator outlines the coverage period for parts and labor, conditions for warranty service, exclusions, and the process for obtaining service. It specifies the warranty terms for both the U.S. and Canada, including the obligations of the original purchaser and the limitations of remedies. The warranty covers manufacturing defects for specified periods and conditions, while exclusions include damage from misuse, unauthorized repairs, and cosmetic damage.

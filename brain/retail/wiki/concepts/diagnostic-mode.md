Diagnostic Mode is a feature in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator that allows technicians to check the operational status and errors of the appliance. This mode is essential for troubleshooting and identifying issues within the refrigerator, enabling efficient repairs and maintenance.

By entering Diagnostic Mode, technicians can access various operational tests and error codes that help diagnose problems effectively.

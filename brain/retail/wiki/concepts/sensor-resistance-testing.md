Sensor Resistance Testing is a method used to check the functionality of sensors in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator by measuring their resistance. This technique is critical for diagnosing sensor-related issues, ensuring that the refrigerator operates correctly.

The repair guide provides specific instructions on how to perform sensor resistance testing, including acceptable resistance values for various sensors.

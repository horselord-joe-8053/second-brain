The Samsung Refrigerator Troubleshooting Guide provides essential information for technicians working with specific models, including the Samsung RF28K9070SG, RF28K9381, and RF28K9380. It details various operational modes such as Demo Mode, Load Mode, and Force Mode/Defrost, along with instructions for each mode. The guide features diagrams of control panels, labeled buttons, and a table of display codes that indicate operational statuses or errors.

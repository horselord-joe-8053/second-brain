# Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide - Page 007
This page provides troubleshooting information for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator model. It outlines various symptoms related to refrigerator issues and corresponding actions to address them.

## Symptoms and Actions
The following table summarizes common symptoms and recommended actions:

| Symptom                                           | Action                                           |
|---------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| The front corners and sides become warm and form condensation. | Check the door seal and ensure it is closing properly. |
| The ice maker does not make ice.                  | Ensure the water tank is filled and check for blockages. |
| The water dispenser is not functioning.            | Inspect for any clogs and ensure the water supply is connected. |

This structured approach aids in diagnosing and resolving common refrigerator problems effectively.

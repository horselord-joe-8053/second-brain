The image on this page provides a visual guide for adjusting the height of the refrigerator doors on the Samsung RF28K9070SG model. It illustrates the use of snap rings in the adjustment process, emphasizing the importance of ensuring proper alignment between the doors. The instructions include checking the height difference and inserting a suitable-sized snap ring between the door and the hinge. A caution note advises users to insert only one snap ring and to keep them out of children's reach. Snap rings come in four sizes: 1 mm, 1.5 mm, 2 mm, and 2.5 mm.

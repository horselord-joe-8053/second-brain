This page provides visual instructions for removing the refrigerator door of the Samsung RF28K9070SG model. It emphasizes safety and proper technique, detailing the steps necessary to safely detach the door for maintenance or repair.

The source includes two illustrations:
1. The first illustration shows the removal of the top hinge, with instructions to "Open the door 90 degrees. While holding the door, pull up and remove the top hinge. Be careful not to damage the connector wires when you remove the hinge."
2. The second illustration depicts the vertical removal of the door, stating, "Gently pull up the door vertically to remove. Do not apply excessive force to the door. The door may fall, causing physical injury."

These instructions are critical for ensuring user safety during maintenance tasks.

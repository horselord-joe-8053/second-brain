# Samsung RF28K9070SG User Manual - Page 053
This page provides visual instructions for inserting a wine rack into the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. It includes two diagrams: one showing the rack being placed under a shelf and another illustrating its positioning in the partition guide of the FlexZone. The instructions emphasize that the wine rack must be empty before insertion to ensure proper placement. Each diagram includes notes for correct positioning, enhancing user understanding.

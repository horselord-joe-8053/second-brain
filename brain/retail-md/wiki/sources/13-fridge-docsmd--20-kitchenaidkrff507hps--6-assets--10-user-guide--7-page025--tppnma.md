The image on this page depicts the user interface for the water dispenser feature of the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS refrigerator. It includes various options available to the user, such as settings for ice mode, light, temperature, and the Measured Fill feature. The display indicates a default volume of 8 ounces for dispensing water, with a range from 1 ounce to 128 ounces. This visual representation highlights the functionality and settings of the refrigerator's water dispenser.

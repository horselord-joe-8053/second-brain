This page provides installation instructions for connecting a water line to the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator. It includes sections titled "BEFORE YOU BEGIN" and "WHAT YOU WILL NEED," detailing recommended copper water supply kits (WX8X2, WX8X3, WX8X4) and the necessary tubing specifications (1/4" outer diameter). A warning is also present, stating, "Connect to potable water supply only. A cold water supply with water pressure between 25 and 120 psi (172 – 827 kilopascals) is required to operate the water dispenser and ice maker."

This page provides a visual installation guide for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, detailing steps 2 through 5 of the installation process. The instructions include pressing the hinge shaft down and positioning the door, connecting bottom connectors as indicated by arrows, attaching the leveling foot cover using a Phillips screwdriver, and repeating the steps for the other door. The combination of visual and textual instructions enhances user comprehension of the installation process.

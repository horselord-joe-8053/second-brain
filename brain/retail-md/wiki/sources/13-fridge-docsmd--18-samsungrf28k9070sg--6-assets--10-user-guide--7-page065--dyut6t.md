This page from the Samsung RF28K9070SG user manual provides troubleshooting information regarding condensation or water drops forming on or around the vertical mullion of the refrigerator. It indicates that this issue may occur due to high surrounding humidity. The troubleshooting table outlines the symptom and the corresponding action to address it.

### Troubleshooting Table
- **Symptom:** Condensation or water drops form on or around the vertical mullion.
- **Action:** While the refrigerator is operating, condensation or water drops may form on or around the vertical mullion if the surrounding humidity is too high.

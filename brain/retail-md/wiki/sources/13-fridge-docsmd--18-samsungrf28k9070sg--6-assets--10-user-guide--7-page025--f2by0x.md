This page provides instructions for re-attaching the fridge doors of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. The process is illustrated with a visual guide that breaks down the steps clearly. 

### Steps for Re-attaching Fridge Doors:
1. Insert the middle hinge shaft into the bottom hole of the door while holding it open at more than 90 degrees.
2. Position the top hinge on the refrigerator and insert the hinge shaft into the top hole of the door, ensuring wires pass through the hinge shaft hole.
3. Insert the clamp in the direction indicated by the arrow.
4. Push back the clamp as demonstrated.

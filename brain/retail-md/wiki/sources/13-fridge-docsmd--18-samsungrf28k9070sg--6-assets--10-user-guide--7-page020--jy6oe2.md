This page provides a visual and textual guide for disconnecting components from the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. Key instructions include disconnecting wire connectors on the left door, a cautionary note against removing both doors simultaneously, and instructions to pull up the clamp for removal. The evidence supporting these instructions is illustrated with step-by-step images that clarify the process, enhancing user understanding and safety.

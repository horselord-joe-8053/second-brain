The image on this page provides a detailed diagram of the GE GNE27JYMFS French door refrigerator, highlighting various features such as the space-saving icemaker, LED lighting, full-width drawer, QuickSpace™ shelf, removable/adjustable door bins, climate zone drawers, and water filter. Each feature is labeled for user understanding, enhancing the usability of the refrigerator.

![The image depicts a diagram of a French door refrigerator, labeled with various features. Key elements include:  1. Space-saving icemaker (on some models) - located on the door. 2. LED lighting - positioned at the top center and sides of the refrigerator compartment. 3. Full-width drawer - located at the bottom of the refrigerator compartment for larger items. 4. QuickSpace™ shelf (on some models) - functions as a normal shelf and slides back for tall items. 5. Removable/adjustable door bins - can be adjusted to accommodate needs. 6.

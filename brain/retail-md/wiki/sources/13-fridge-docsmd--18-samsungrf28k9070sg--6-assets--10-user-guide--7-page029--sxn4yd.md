The image on this page provides essential instructions for the installation of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, specifically focusing on the flooring protection during the installation process. It emphasizes the importance of placing cardboard under each leg of the refrigerator to prevent floor damage and advises against unnecessary movement once the refrigerator is positioned. Additionally, it includes a note about removing the refrigerator doors if the appliance cannot fit through the entrance due to size constraints.

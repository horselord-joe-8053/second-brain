The source provides visual instructions for adjusting and replacing refrigerator shelves, specifically focusing on the "Tuck Away Shelf" feature. Key steps include tilting the shelf to remove it, ensuring hooks are engaged for stability, and extending the shelf by pulling it outward. The evidence supporting these instructions is presented visually through the image, which illustrates the steps clearly.

This page of the KitchenAid® KRFF507HPS user guide provides detailed instructions for adjusting the temperature settings of the refrigerator and freezer compartments. It specifies a recommended freezer temperature range of -5°F to 5°F (-21°C to -15°C) and outlines the steps for making these adjustments, including pressing the LOCK and TEMPERATURE buttons. The page also introduces the Max Cool feature, which temporarily lowers the temperature during periods of high usage, and includes a chart for temperature adjustments based on specific conditions, such as "REFRIGERATOR too cold" or "FREEZER too warm."

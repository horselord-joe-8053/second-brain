This page provides a visual guide for detaching the refrigerator doors of the Samsung RF28K9070SG. The diagram illustrates the location of three screws that must be removed using a Phillips screwdriver, along with instructions to pull up and remove the top cover after unscrewing. The combination of visual and textual instructions makes it easy for users to follow the steps necessary for door detachment.

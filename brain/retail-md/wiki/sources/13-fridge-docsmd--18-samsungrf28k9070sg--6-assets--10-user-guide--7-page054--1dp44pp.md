# Samsung RF28K9070SG User Manual - Page 054
This page provides instructions for using the sliding shelf in the FlexZone of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, specifically for inserting a wine rack. 

## Instructions
- Insert the wine rack on top of the sliding shelf of the FlexZone, normally below the partition guide.

## Cautionary Notes
- Do not store wine bottles in the freezer.
- Avoid stacking wine bottles in two rows.
- Do not use bottles taller than 330 mm, as these actions may cause physical injury.

Visual aids accompany these instructions to enhance understanding and ensure safety.

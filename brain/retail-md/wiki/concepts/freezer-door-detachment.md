# Freezer Door Detachment

Freezer door detachment is the process of safely removing the freezer doors from the refrigerator unit, specifically for models like the Samsung RF28K9070SG. This procedure is essential for maintenance or repair tasks.

## Steps for Detachment
1. Remove the fridge doors before removing the freezer doors.
2. Use a Phillips screwdriver to remove screws and access necessary components.
3. Disconnect wire connectors and locate the hinge shaft to safely detach the door.

**Safety Precautions:** Follow the steps carefully to avoid any risks during the detachment process.

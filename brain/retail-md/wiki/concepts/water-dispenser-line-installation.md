Water Dispenser Line Installation refers to the process of connecting a water line to the refrigerator's water dispenser. This procedure is essential for the functionality of the refrigerator's water and ice dispensing features. The installation involves specific steps, including shutting off the main water source, inserting a pipe clamp and shut-off valve, and connecting the water line installation kit.

The Measured Fill Feature is a functionality in the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS refrigerator that allows users to dispense a specific amount of water using a measuring cup. The user interface indicates a default volume of 8 ounces, with options ranging from 1 ounce to 128 ounces. This feature enhances user convenience by allowing precise control over the amount of water dispensed.

The user guide provides detailed instructions on calibrating this feature, emphasizing the importance of flushing the water system before calibration and outlining the necessary steps for proper operation.

# Water Line Connection Instructions  
Water line connection instructions refer to the process of connecting a water supply line to a refrigerator, which is essential for enabling the water dispenser and ice maker functionalities. Proper installation involves turning off the water supply and using the correct tubing size, typically a minimum of 1/2" (12.7 mm). These instructions are crucial for ensuring the refrigerator operates effectively and avoids potential issues.

## Key Steps  
The GE GNE27JYMFS user guide provides specific details on this process, emphasizing the importance of connecting to a potable water supply and adhering to the required water pressure specifications. Key steps include:  
- Fastening the shutoff valve  
- Tightening the pipe clamp  
- Routing the tubing  
- Flushing out the tubing  

There is an emphasis on compliance with Massachusetts Plumbing Codes and warnings against over-tightening.

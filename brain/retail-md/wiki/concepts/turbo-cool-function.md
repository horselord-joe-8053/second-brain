# Turbo Cool Function
The Turbo Cool function is a feature of the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator that rapidly cools the refrigerator compartment. This function is significant as it enhances the cooling efficiency, especially after the door has been opened frequently or when adding a large amount of food. During its operation, the refrigerator temperature cannot be changed.

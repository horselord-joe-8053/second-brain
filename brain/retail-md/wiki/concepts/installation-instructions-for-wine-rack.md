# Installation Instructions for Wine Rack
This concept outlines the procedure for inserting a wine rack into the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. The instructions specify that the wine rack must be empty before insertion to ensure proper placement. Users are guided through the process with visual aids that illustrate the correct positioning of the rack under a shelf and in the partition guide of the FlexZone.

Refrigerator Door Detachment is the process of removing the refrigerator doors for maintenance or repair. This procedure is essential for users needing to access internal components or transport the refrigerator. The Samsung RF28K9070SG user manual provides specific instructions and visual aids to assist users in safely detaching the doors.

Levelling Feet Adjustment is the process of adjusting the height of a refrigerator using its levelling feet to ensure it is stable and properly aligned. This technique is essential for the proper installation of the refrigerator to prevent damage or operational issues. The user guide for the Samsung RF28K9070SG provides specific instructions for this adjustment.

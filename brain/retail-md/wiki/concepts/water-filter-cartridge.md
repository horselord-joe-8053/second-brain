# Water Filter Cartridge (model XWFE)
The Water Filter Cartridge (model XWFE) is a replaceable component in the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator that filters water for the ice and water dispenser. Regular replacement is crucial for maintaining water quality.

## Replacement Instructions
- Locate the water filter.
- If a replacement cartridge is unavailable, use a filter bypass plug.
- To install the new filter, slowly turn it to the right until it stops and press the RESET WATER FILTER button for 3 seconds.

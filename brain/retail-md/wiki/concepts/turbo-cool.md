# Turbo Cool
Turbo Cool is a feature of the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator that enhances cooling performance by rapidly lowering the temperature in the refrigerator compartment. This feature is particularly useful after loading the refrigerator with new groceries, ensuring that food is quickly brought to the optimal storage temperature.

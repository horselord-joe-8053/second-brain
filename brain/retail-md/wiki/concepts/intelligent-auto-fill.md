# Intelligent Auto Fill
Intelligent Auto Fill is a new water-dispensing technology introduced by KitchenAid in its latest refrigerator models. This feature allows for hands-free water dispensing, automatically filling containers of various sizes without the need for manual operation. It represents a significant advancement in water-dispensing technology, although it is not available in the KRFF507HPS model.

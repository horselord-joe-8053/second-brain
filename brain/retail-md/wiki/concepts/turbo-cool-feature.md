The Turbo Cool feature in the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator enables rapid cooling after the door has been opened. This function is designed to quickly restore optimal temperatures, enhancing the overall user experience and food preservation. The Turbo Cool feature enhances the efficiency of the refrigerator and is a significant selling point for users.

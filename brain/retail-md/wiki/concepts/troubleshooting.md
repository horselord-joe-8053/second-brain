# Troubleshooting  
Troubleshooting is a systematic approach to diagnosing and resolving problems with appliances. In the context of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, it involves identifying issues related to the Samsung Smart Home app and implementing corrective actions. This concept is particularly relevant for users of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, as the user manual provides specific guidance on common issues, including the formation of condensation due to high humidity.

## Importance  
Effective troubleshooting enhances user experience by providing clear steps to resolve connectivity and functionality issues with the refrigerator.

## Common Issues  
Les instructions de dépannage pour le réfrigérateur Samsung RF28K9070SG aident les utilisateurs à résoudre des problèmes courants, tels que :  
- La condensation  
- Le fonctionnement de la machine à glace  
- Les odeurs désagréables

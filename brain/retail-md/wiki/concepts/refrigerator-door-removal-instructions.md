# Refrigerator Door Removal Instructions  
Refrigerator Door Removal Instructions are a set of guidelines detailing the steps required to safely remove the doors of a refrigerator, specifically for the GE GNE27JYMFS model. The instructions include specific steps labeled H through Q, tools required (5/16” socket/ratchet driver, 1/4” Allen wrench), and torque specifications (60 in-lb or 6.78 Nm) for screws.  

These instructions are crucial for users attempting to install or service the refrigerator, ensuring safety and proper handling. The guidelines emphasize the importance of unplugging the refrigerator and using the correct tools to avoid hazards.

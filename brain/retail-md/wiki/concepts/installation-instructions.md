## GE GNE27JYMFS Specific Guidelines  
The GE GNE27JYMFS user guide includes detailed instructions for two types of refrigerator handles: a slide-on type and a handle with set-screws. It emphasizes the importance of using the correct tools and following the steps carefully to avoid damage or improper installation.

Key steps for the installation of the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator include:
- Unplugging wire connectors.
- Removing screws with a Phillips screwdriver.
- Using a 5/16” socket/rachet driver to remove bolts.

These instructions are crucial for ensuring that the appliance is installed correctly and functions as intended.

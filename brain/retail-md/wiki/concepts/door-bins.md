Door Bins are removable storage compartments located on the doors of refrigerators, including the KitchenAid® French Door Refrigerator (Model KRFF507HPS) and the Samsung RF28K9070SG. They enhance organization and accessibility of items, making it easier for users to store and retrieve frequently used products.  

In the context of the Samsung RF28K9070SG, the user manual provides a diagram illustrating the types and sizes of door bins, emphasizing their importance for proper organization and functionality within the appliance. Proper instructions for the removal of door bins are crucial for maintenance and safe operation of the refrigerator.

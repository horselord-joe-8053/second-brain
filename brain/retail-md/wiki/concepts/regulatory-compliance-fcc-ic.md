# Regulatory Compliance (FCC/IC)
Regulatory compliance refers to adherence to regulations set by the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) and Industry Canada (IC) regarding electronic devices. This compliance ensures that the device operates within legal standards for safety and interference, which is important for consumer safety and product legitimacy.

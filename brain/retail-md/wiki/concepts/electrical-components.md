# Electrical Components

Electrical components are parts such as sensors, motors, and connectors that facilitate the operation of electrical devices. Understanding these components is vital for effective maintenance and repair of appliances like the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator.

Key components include:
- **Sensors:** Humidity sensor, ice maker sensor
- **Motors:** Auger motor, fan motors
- **Connectors:** Various connectors used in the wiring diagram

The Tuck Away Shelf is a feature on some refrigerator models that can be adjusted or retracted for convenience. Instructions for its operation include tilting the shelf to remove it, ensuring hooks are engaged for stability, and extending the shelf by pulling it outward. Proper engagement of hooks is emphasized for safety and functionality.

# Vegetable Shelf Maintenance
Vegetable Shelf Maintenance involves specific instructions for handling and maintaining the vegetable shelf in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. Proper maintenance ensures optimal use of space and functionality.

The maintenance guide includes the following steps:

1. Slide out the left-side vegetable drawer.
2. Slide in the right-side foldable shelf.
3. While supporting the vegetable shelf with one hand, slide it out with the other hand.

These instructions are crucial for users to effectively manage the vegetable shelf.

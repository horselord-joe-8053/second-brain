# Normal Operating Conditions
Normal Operating Conditions refer to the guidelines and explanations regarding the typical sounds and operations of the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator. This concept is essential for users to understand what sounds are normal, helping to alleviate concerns about potential malfunctions. The user guide categorizes sounds into sections such as "CLICK, POPS, CRACKS AND SNAPS" and "WATER SOUNDS," providing clarity and reassurance.

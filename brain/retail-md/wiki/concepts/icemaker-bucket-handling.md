# Icemaker Bucket Handling
Icemaker Bucket Handling refers to the instructions for the proper removal, emptying, and repositioning of the icemaker bucket in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. This process is essential for maintaining the icemaker effectively and safely. The handling instructions emphasize safety precautions to prevent injury or damage during the operation.

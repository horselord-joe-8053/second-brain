# 4-Way Valve Operation
The 4-Way Valve is a mechanism that directs refrigerant flow in refrigeration systems, allowing for the transition between heating and cooling modes. Its operation is fundamental to the effective functioning of refrigerators, particularly in models like the Samsung RF28K9070SG.

# Error Codes  
Error Codes are specific numerical indicators that represent various issues within the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator's systems. These codes help technicians and users identify problems, such as sensor errors or component failures. They are essential for diagnosing issues within appliances, providing specific diagnostic methods and resistance/voltage values necessary for troubleshooting.

In the context of the Samsung RF28K9070SG, error codes 81 to 85 relate to the compressor system and provide essential information for troubleshooting and repair processes. Understanding these error codes is crucial for effective appliance repair.

# Auger Motor Assembly Removal

Auger Motor Assembly Removal is the procedure for detaching the auger motor from the ice maker in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. Understanding how to remove this component is crucial for troubleshooting and repairs related to ice production.

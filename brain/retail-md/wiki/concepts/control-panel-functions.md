# Control Panel Functions
Control panel functions refer to the various controls available on the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator's panel. These include temperature settings, water filter reset, alarm, lock, LED dispenser light, and the Turbo Cool feature. Understanding these functions is essential for users to operate the refrigerator effectively.

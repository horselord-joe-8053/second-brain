The Max Cool feature is a functionality in the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS refrigerator that temporarily lowers the temperature in both the refrigerator and freezer compartments. This feature is particularly useful during periods of high usage, helping to maintain optimal cooling performance. Instructions for activating and deactivating the Max Cool feature are included in the user guide.

# Handling Tempered Glass Shelves
Tempered glass shelves are designed for durability and safety in refrigerators. Proper handling is crucial to prevent accidents and damage. This includes specific instructions for removing and reinstalling the shelves, as well as safety precautions regarding their weight and the risk of scratching the surface with glass containers.

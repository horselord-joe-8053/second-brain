# MAC Address

A MAC address (Media Access Control address) is a unique identifier assigned to network interfaces for communications at the data link layer. In the context of smart appliances like the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, understanding the MAC address is important for Wi-Fi connectivity and troubleshooting network issues.

# Ice Bucket Removal  

Ice Bucket Removal refers to the process of detaching the ice bucket from the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator's ice maker. This is a common maintenance task that users may need to perform, and clear instructions are essential for proper execution.  

The instructions for removing the ice bucket typically involve three steps: holding the bucket, lifting it, and removing any remaining ice cubes before replacing it. Proper maintenance of the ice bucket is essential for optimal refrigerator performance, ensuring the ice maker functions correctly.

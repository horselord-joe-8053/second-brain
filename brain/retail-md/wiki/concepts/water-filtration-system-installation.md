# Water Filtration System Installation
Water filtration system installation refers to the process of setting up a water filter in a refrigerator to ensure the delivery of safe drinking water. This process is crucial for the functionality of the appliance and the health of its users.

## Installation Steps
1. Locate the water filter.
2. Remove any packaging.
3. Align the filter with the housing.
4. Turn the filter clockwise 90 degrees to secure it.

### Safety Considerations
- Avoid using microbiologically unsafe water.
- It is important to flush the water system after installation to ensure cleanliness and safety.

# Temperature Settings  
Temperature settings refer to the adjustable controls for the freezer and refrigerator compartments of various models, including the GE GNE27JYMFS, KitchenAid KRFF507HPS, and Samsung RF28K9070SG. These settings are typically measured in Fahrenheit or Celsius and are crucial for maintaining the proper operation of the appliances. Proper temperature management is essential for food preservation and safety.

## GE GNE27JYMFS  
The user guide for the GE GNE27JYMFS provides instructions on how to adjust these settings effectively.

## KitchenAid KRFF507HPS  
The KitchenAid KRFF507HPS user guide emphasizes the significance of setting the correct temperature. Users are advised to wait 24 hours before placing food in the refrigerator to ensure that it reaches the desired cooling level. Additionally, it provides specific instructions on how to adjust these settings, including a recommended freezer temperature range of -5°F to 5°F (-21°C to -15°C).

## Samsung RF28K9070SG  
Temperature settings on the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator allow users to set and monitor the temperature for both the freezer and fridge compartments. The recommended settings are -8°F for the freezer and 89°F for the fridge, which are crucial for optimal food preservation. These settings can be managed through the control panel of the refrigerator and are essential for maintaining food safety and appliance efficiency.

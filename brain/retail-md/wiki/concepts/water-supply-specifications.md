# Water Supply Specifications
Water Supply Specifications refer to the guidelines regarding the quality and conditions of water supplied to the water filter cartridge. These specifications are critical for ensuring the proper functionality and safety of the water filtration system in refrigerators.

## Key Guidelines
- **Service Flow:** 0.5 gpm (1.89 lpm)
- **Water Pressure:** 25-120 psi (172-827 kPa)
- **Water Temperature:** 33°F-100°F (0.6°C-38°C)

Adhering to these specifications is essential for optimal filter performance and safety.

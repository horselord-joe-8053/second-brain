Door height adjustment is the process of modifying the height of refrigerator doors to ensure they align properly. This procedure is particularly relevant for the Samsung RF28K9070SG model, where snap rings are used to facilitate the adjustment. The user manual provides visual instructions on how to perform this adjustment, highlighting the importance of using the correct size snap ring and ensuring safety precautions are followed.

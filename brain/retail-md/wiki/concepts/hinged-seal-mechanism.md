The Hinged Seal Mechanism is a critical component of refrigerator doors that ensures a tight seal when the door is closed. This mechanism enhances cooling efficiency by preventing cold air from escaping and warm air from entering. The user guide for the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS illustrates how this mechanism operates, highlighting its importance in maintaining optimal refrigerator performance.

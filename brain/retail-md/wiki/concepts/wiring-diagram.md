# Wiring Diagram

A wiring diagram is a schematic representation of the electrical connections and components within a device. It provides essential information for troubleshooting and repairing appliances, such as the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator.

The diagram includes various components like connectors, sensors, and motors, illustrating their roles and connections within the appliance.

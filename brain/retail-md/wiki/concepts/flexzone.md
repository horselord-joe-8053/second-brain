# FlexZone
The FlexZone is a customizable compartment in the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator that can be adjusted for different storage needs. This feature is particularly useful for wine storage, with specific instructions provided for its use. Safety precautions are emphasized to prevent physical injury when utilizing this compartment.

# Temperature Controls
Temperature controls are mechanisms in the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator that allow users to set and adjust the temperature for both the freezer and refrigerator compartments. Proper adjustment of these settings is crucial for optimal food preservation, ensuring that perishable items are stored at safe temperatures.

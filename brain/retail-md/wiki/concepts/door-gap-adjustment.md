# Door Gap Adjustment

Door Gap Adjustment is a procedure to correct the gap between the refrigerator doors, ensuring proper sealing and functionality. This adjustment is crucial for maintaining the refrigerator's efficiency and preventing energy loss. The Samsung RF28K9070SG user manual provides specific instructions for performing this adjustment, including the necessary tools and steps to follow.

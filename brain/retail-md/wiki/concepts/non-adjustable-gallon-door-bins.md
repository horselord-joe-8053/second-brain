# Non-Adjustable Gallon Door Bins
Non-Adjustable Gallon Door Bins are fixed bins in the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator designed specifically to hold gallon-sized containers. Understanding these bins helps users recognize the limitations and capabilities of their refrigerator's storage options, ensuring effective organization.

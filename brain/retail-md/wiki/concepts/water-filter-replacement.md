Water filter replacement is the process of removing and replacing the water filter in a refrigerator to ensure a clean water supply. This task is essential for maintaining the quality and hygiene of water dispensed by the refrigerator.  

Specific instructions for water filter replacement are detailed in the user guides for both the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS and Samsung RF28K9070SG models. The Samsung manual emphasizes the importance of using only Samsung-provided or approved filters to maintain the functionality of the refrigerator's water dispensing system.  

For the Samsung RF28K9070SG model, the user manual provides a clear, illustrated guide on how to perform the task of removing an old water filter cartridge and installing a new one, ensuring users can maintain their refrigerator effectively.

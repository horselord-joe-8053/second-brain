# Freezer Drawer Removal Instructions
The Freezer Drawer Removal Instructions provide a set of steps and visual aids designed to guide users in safely removing the freezer drawer from the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator. This process is essential for users needing to access the freezer compartment for cleaning or maintenance. The instructions emphasize safety, recommending the use of a non-scratching work surface to prevent damage during the removal process.

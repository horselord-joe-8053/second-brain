# Refrigerator Installation Instructions  
Refrigerator installation instructions provide guidelines for properly installing a refrigerator, including location, clearance, leveling, and handle attachment. These instructions are crucial for ensuring the refrigerator operates efficiently and safely.

## Key Guidelines  
- Install the refrigerator in temperatures between 60°F (16°C) and 100°F (37°C).  
- Maintain proper clearance around the refrigerator for optimal operation.  
- Ensure handles are securely attached to prevent accidents.  
- Level and position the refrigerator correctly to ensure optimal functionality and door alignment.

Contaminant Reduction is the process of decreasing the concentration of harmful substances in water. The performance data for the XWFE water filter cartridge provides specific information on various contaminants and their reduction percentages, which is essential for consumers assessing water quality and safety.  

Additionally, the PW9B2L/P9RFWB2L water filtration system used in the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS refrigerator demonstrates effective contaminant reduction, as evidenced by performance data that outlines specific contaminants and their reduction percentages.

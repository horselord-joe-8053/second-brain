# Limited Warranty  
A limited warranty is a guarantee provided by the manufacturer that covers defects in materials or workmanship for a specified period. This concept is crucial for consumer protection, as it outlines the terms under which a product can be replaced or repaired.  

The GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator, for example, comes with a limited warranty that covers defects in material or workmanship for one year, with exclusions for damages caused by accidents or improper installation.

# Samsung  
Samsung is a major South Korean multinational conglomerate headquartered in South Korea, known for its diverse range of products, including consumer electronics and home appliances. The company is recognized for its innovation and quality in the appliance industry, particularly with products like the RF28K9070SG refrigerator. 

Samsung is the manufacturer of the refrigerator model RF28K9070SG, which is the focus of the user guide and product information for its appliances. The company plays a central role in providing user guides and troubleshooting information to assist customers in properly using and maintaining their products. Additionally, Samsung is responsible for announcements related to open-source software associated with its products.

Samsung contributes significantly to advancements in technology and user convenience within the home appliance sector. The company also provides customer support for its products, ensuring users have access to assistance and resources.

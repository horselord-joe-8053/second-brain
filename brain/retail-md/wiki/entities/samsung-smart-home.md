# Samsung Smart Home  
Samsung Smart Home is an application designed to enhance the user experience with Samsung appliances, including the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. The app allows users to connect and control their appliances, providing functionalities for monitoring and managing home appliances remotely.

## Troubleshooting Features  
The app provides specific troubleshooting actions for various symptoms related to the refrigerator, improving connectivity and functionality.

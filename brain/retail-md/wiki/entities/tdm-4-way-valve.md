# TDM 4 Way Valve
The TDM 4 Way Valve is a critical component in refrigeration systems, responsible for directing refrigerant flow to facilitate both heating and cooling cycles. Understanding its operation is essential for troubleshooting and repairing refrigerators like the Samsung RF28K9070SG.

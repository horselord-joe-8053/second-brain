The XWFE Water Filter Cartridge is designed for use with GE refrigerators, specifically engineered to reduce contaminants in drinking water. It is noted for its high performance, effectively reducing various harmful substances, including Chlorine Taste and Odor, Cysts, Benzene, and Mercury, with reduction percentages typically at or above 98%.

Snap rings are components used in the adjustment of refrigerator doors, specifically in the Samsung RF28K9070SG model. They play a crucial role in ensuring that the doors are properly aligned. The user manual specifies that snap rings come in four sizes: 1 mm, 1.5 mm, 2 mm, and 2.5 mm, and emphasizes the importance of using the correct size for effective adjustment.

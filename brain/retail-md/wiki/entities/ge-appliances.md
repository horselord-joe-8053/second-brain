# GE Appliances  
GE Appliances is a prominent manufacturer of home appliances, including refrigerators, ovens, and dishwashers. The company is known for its innovation in appliance technology and has a significant presence in the consumer market.  

## Products  
The company manufactures the GE GNE27JYMFS refrigerator, which has been noted for its features and performance. GE Appliances also produces the Water Filter Cartridge XWFE, enhancing the functionality of their refrigerators.

## Consumer Support  
GE Appliances focuses on delivering quality products that enhance consumer convenience and safety. They provide installation instructions and support for their products, ensuring proper setup and functionality for users. Additionally, GE Appliances is responsible for providing warranty terms for its products, which play a significant role in consumer rights by protecting customers against defects and malfunctions in their appliances.

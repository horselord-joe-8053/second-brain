# Brazil Election 2026 Summary (First Round Results)

**Date:** October 6, 2026

## Overview
The first round of the 2026 Brazilian presidential election concluded on Sunday, October 4, 2026. Neither incumbent President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva nor challenger Senator Flávio Bolsonaro (Liberal Party) secured the 50% majority required to win in the first round, forcing a runoff election.

## Key Results
*   **Flávio Bolsonaro (PL):** ~47.03% of valid votes (First place).
*   **Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva (PT):** ~45.16% of valid votes (Second place).
*   **Runoff Date:** Scheduled for **October 25, 2026**.

## Context
*   The election is polarizing, mirroring the 2022 contest between Lula and Jair Bolsonaro.
*   Flávio Bolsonaro is the son of former President Jair Bolsonaro, who is currently serving a prison sentence under house arrest for plotting to overturn the 2022 election results.
*   The results indicate a notable rightward shift in Brazilian politics, with the PL party performing strongly in congressional races.
*   Key issues for voters include the economy, personal debt, crime and insecurity, and Brazil’s foreign policy alignment (with Bolsonaro favoring closer ties to the U.S. and Lula maintaining distance).

## Next Steps
Both campaigns are now entering a three-week push to capture moderate voters and those who supported minor candidates in the first round.

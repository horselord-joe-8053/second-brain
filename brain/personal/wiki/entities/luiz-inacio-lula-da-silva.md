Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, commonly known as Lula, is the incumbent President of Brazil and a major candidate in the 2026 presidential election. He represents the Workers' Party (PT) and has been a central figure in Brazilian politics, particularly in the context of the recent elections. Lula is seeking re-election in the 2026 presidential runoff. His coalition-building efforts are crucial for his campaign as he aims to regain support from diverse voter bases.

In addition to his domestic political agenda, Lula is expected to strengthen Brazil's ties with China, enhancing diplomatic and economic relations between the two countries.

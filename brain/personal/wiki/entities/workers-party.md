The Workers' Party (PT) is a significant political party in Brazil, representing the interests of Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva in the 2026 presidential election. The party has been a central player in Brazilian politics for decades.  

The PT is also facing anti-PT sentiment, which influences voter consolidation around the Liberal Party (PL). Understanding the dynamics of the PT is crucial for analyzing the current political landscape.

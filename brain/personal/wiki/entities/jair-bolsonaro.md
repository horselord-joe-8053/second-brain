# Jair Bolsonaro  
Jair Bolsonaro is a Brazilian politician and former president, representing the Liberal Party (PL). He is a challenger in the 2026 presidential runoff, having gained momentum from the first round of voting. Bolsonaro's campaign focuses on leveraging the rightward shift in Brazilian politics and addressing key voter concerns.  

Bolsonaro's conviction has significantly impacted the political landscape. His political legacy continues to influence the dynamics of the Liberal Party (PL) and the broader electoral environment in Brazil.

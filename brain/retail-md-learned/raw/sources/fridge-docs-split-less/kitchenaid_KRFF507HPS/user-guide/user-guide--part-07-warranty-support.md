### KA-WARRANTY-001 — Ten-year limited refrigerator warranty structure and exclusions

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-WARRANTY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 41.  
**Customer phrasing:** How long is KitchenAid warranty?; Proof of purchase needed?; Who performs warranty service?.  

**Warranty administration:** Receipt/proof of purchase, model and serial number and detailed problem description are requested. U.S. contact printed here: **1-800-422-1230**; Canada: **1-800-807-6777**. Authorized KitchenAid Service Providers conduct covered warranty work.

**Coverage structure:** The page identifies a **one-year limited warranty (parts and labor)** for covered manufacturing defects and additional **limited sealed-system warranty terms** within the printed “Ten Year Limited Warranty.” Different periods/covered components must be read from the source table before promising a replacement.

**Not automatically covered:** The printed exclusion list includes commercial/multi-family uses, improper maintenance/installation or external plumbing/electrical corrections. Eligibility depends on country, receipt and full conditions. Do not infer that any cosmetic or installation defect is covered.

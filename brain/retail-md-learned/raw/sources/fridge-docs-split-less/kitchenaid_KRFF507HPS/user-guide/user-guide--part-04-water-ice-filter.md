### KA-WATER-FILTER-001 — Installing and replacing refrigerator water filters

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-WATER-FILTER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 17, 18.  
**Customer phrasing:** Replace KitchenAid water filter; Filter status light; Clogged water filter.  

**What it means:** The source explains the water filter holder, filter status display, filter replacement and flushing procedure. It recommends a replacement about **every six months** and checking correct cartridge seating when flow is reduced.

**General procedure:** Locate the water-filter housing, release the old cartridge by the illustrated quarter-turn mechanism and remove it. Fit the new cartridge, turn to lock, flush per the printed water-dispensing procedure and reset the filter-status indicator by the method appropriate to the installed controls.

**Do not:** Substitute an unverified model filter or treat a filter-indicator reset as proof that contamination was removed.

**Original PDF visual reference:** The **unlabeled filter-housing and cartridge illustrations** under “Water Filtration System” on printed pages 17–18 show the locking direction and correct seating.

### KA-FILTER-PERFORMANCE-001 — Certified water filtration capacity, limits and contaminants

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-FILTER-PERFORMANCE-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 40.  
**Customer phrasing:** P9RFWB2L gallons capacity; What NSF certifications does filter have?; Does filter remove lead?.  

**Certified configuration:** Water filtration system **P9WB2L / P9RFWB2L**, rated capacity **200 gallons (757 liters)**. The printed performance sheet describes tests/certifications against **NSF/ANSI 42, 53 and 401** and **CSA B483.1** under specified challenge concentrations.

**Selected printed performance examples:** Chlorine taste/odor **97.0% average reduction**; Particulate Class I **greater than 99.9%**; lead at pH 6.5 / 8.5 **greater than 99.3% / 98.6%**; cyst reduction **greater than 99.99%**, all in the manufacturer's specified laboratory context. These are not unconditional promises for arbitrary contaminants or water systems.

### KA-ICE-MAKER-001 — Ice maker operation and ice-storage-bin maintenance

**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 26, 27.  
**Customer phrasing:** Ice maker makes no ice; Remove KitchenAid ice bucket; How to turn on ice maker?.  

**What it covers:** The manual distinguishes ice-maker control, removable storage bin and the expected initial/water-dependent production behavior. Poor ice production can result from the documented water-supply, filter, door closure, new-installation or ice-bin conditions; a slow ice rate alone does not establish a failed motor.

**Customer route:** Confirm ice-maker ON status, water supply, door closure and ice-storage-bin seating; use the printed instructions for removal and cleaning without using sharp objects inside the ice maker.

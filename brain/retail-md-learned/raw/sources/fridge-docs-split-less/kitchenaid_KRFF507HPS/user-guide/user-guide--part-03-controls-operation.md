### KA-TEMP-CONTROLS-001 — Fridge/freezer temperature controls and cooling on/off

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-TEMP-CONTROLS-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 21, 22.  
**Customer phrasing:** Cooling is off but lights work; Recommended fridge temperature; How to adjust freezer settings?.  

**Setup:** The refrigerator has electronically operated temperature controls that display actual/set temperatures according to fitted control type. The manual provides recommended settings and the procedure to adjust toward warmer/colder after allowing time for stabilization.

**Cooling ON/OFF:** A powered display or light does not necessarily mean that refrigeration is enabled. The guide explicitly notes that when Cooling is OFF the refrigerator and freezer **will not cool**; identify this state before diagnosing a hardware failure.

**Model boundary:** Verify the actual panel buttons/controls before supplying a hold sequence because features such as door-alarm, measured fill, Sabbath or temperature-controlled pantry vary by fitment.

### KA-MAX-ICE-SABBATH-001 — Max Ice, Sabbath Mode and power-outage indicator

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-MAX-ICE-SABBATH-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 22, 23.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to use Sabbath Mode?; Max Ice feature; Power Outage indicator went off.  

**Functions:** The manual describes **Max Ice** for temporarily increasing ice production, a **Sabbath Mode** for qualifying control variants and a **Power Outage** indication that informs the user of a past electrical interruption. These states are not interchangeable with cooling being OFF.

**Important Sabbath limitation:** The manual says a power outage that happens during Sabbath Mode may not be indicated, so the absence of a power-outage icon in that mode cannot prove uninterrupted power.

**Control procedure:** Use the exact illustrated button combination for the fitted panel; the source distinguishes mode changes and exit actions.

### KA-SHELVES-001 — Adjustable shelf frames and tuck-away shelf

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-SHELVES-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 28.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to remove refrigerator shelf?; Tuck Away shelf instructions.  

**Removal:** For a shelf/frame, lift the front and release rear hooks from supports; reinstall by guiding the rear hooks into the supports, raising the front until hooks seat and lowering the front fully.

**Model variants:** The source identifies a **Tuck Away Shelf** on some models; retract/extend the front portion using its dedicated mechanism.

**Safety:** Shelves/covers are heavy tempered glass. Use both hands, avoid impact and do not pour warm water onto cold glass.

### KA-CRISPERS-PANTRY-001 — Crispers, controlled drawer and cover removal

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-CRISPERS-PANTRY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 29.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to remove crisper cover?; Can pantry temperature control whole fridge?.  

**Crispers:** The manual states crisper humidity is **automatically regulated**, so this version has no user humidity slider for those drawers. Slide a crisper halfway, lift it out, then reinstall onto slides past the stops. A drawer accidentally pulled fully to the stop may need careful repositioning.

**Cover:** Remove drawers, hold the glass insert firmly, raise it in the center and slide it forward, then lift out the frame. Reinstall the frame into side supports and slide the glass insert into place.

**Temperature-controlled pantry:** On equipped models, the control adjusts **that drawer only**; it does not set or turn off the whole refrigerator/freezer.

### KA-CLEANING-001 — Interior, exterior and stainless-steel cleaning restrictions

**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 31.  
**Customer phrasing:** What cleaner is safe for stainless steel?; How often to clean refrigerator?.  

**Routine cleaning:** The automatic-defrost refrigerator and freezer should be cleaned about **once a month** to control odor and spills should be wiped promptly.

**Finish-specific precautions:** Use a soft clean cloth and warm soapy water for suitable stainless surfaces; avoid steel-wool, abrasive cleaners and inappropriate chemicals. **Important:** Cleaning advice for smooth/painted finish and stainless finish can differ; check which finish is fitted.

### KA-CONDENSER-001 — Condenser cleaning, LEDs, holidays and moving

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-CONDENSER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 32.  
**Customer phrasing:** How often should I clean condenser?; LED lights not turning on; Vacation shutdown.  

**Condenser:** Routine cleaning is **not normally necessary** under ordinary home conditions. In particularly dusty/greasy conditions or with significant pet traffic, the guide recommends cleaning **every 2–3 months**. Unplug, remove the base grille, vacuum accessible grille/openings/front condenser with a soft brush, replace grille and reconnect.

**Lighting:** Refrigerator/freezer LEDs are described as not needing routine replacement; if they do not light on door opening, contact service.

**Extended absence / relocation:** The manual provides distinct rules for leaving cooling on, shutting down and moving, including perishable food, water supply/ice maker and cleaning/door position. Follow the appropriate listed branch rather than giving one instruction for all cases.

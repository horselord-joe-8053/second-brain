## Safety, electrical precautions and service boundaries

**Original manual:** *French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide*. **Source file:** `user-guide.pdf`.

### KA-SAFETY-001 — Critical user, electrical, child entrapment and glass safety

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-SAFETY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 3, 4, 7, 12.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to disconnect power for cleaning?; What is the required voltage?; Moving refrigerator safely.  

**Safety categories:** The manual contains hazard symbols and explicit electrical-shock, excessive-weight, explosion, and child-entrapment warnings. Installation or moving a heavy refrigerator requires **two or more people**. When discarding an old refrigerator, follow the printed door/shelf instructions to prevent a child from becoming trapped.

**Electrical supply:** A **115 V, 60 Hz, AC-only, 15 or 20 A fused, grounded supply** is required. Use a grounded **three-prong outlet**, preferably a separate circuit, not an extension cord, adapter, removed ground prong or switched outlet. Before installation, cleaning or light work, set Cooling OFF and disconnect power.

**Glass:** Do not clean cold glass shelves with warm water; abrupt temperature changes or impact may break glass. Tempered glass can shatter into pebble-size pieces; support shelves with both hands.

**Customer support boundary:** Do not propose internal wiring changes or bypass grounding as a troubleshooting option.

### UNKNOWN-KA-COMPONENT-VOLTAGES — Board-level repair tests and part interchange not documented

**Knowledge ID:** `UNKNOWN-KA-COMPONENT-VOLTAGES`  
**Evidence:** [UNKNOWN]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 12, 32, 41.  
**Customer phrasing:** Can I test inverter board voltage?; Are parts compatible with another KitchenAid model?.  

**What IS documented:** Safe user controls, electrical supply requirements, basic maintenance checks and approved-service routes are provided.

**What is NOT established:** The owner guide does not supply a comprehensive inverter-board voltage diagnostic table, board-level soldering procedure or an authoritative component interchangeability matrix for other refrigerator model suffixes.

**Safe response:** Give the applicable household checks and refer hazardous internal diagnostics to authorized service; do not invent test voltages or compatible replacement parts.

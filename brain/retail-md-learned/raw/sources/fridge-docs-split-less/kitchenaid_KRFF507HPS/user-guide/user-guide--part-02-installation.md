### KA-UNPACK-001 — Unpacking, placement and moving the refrigerator

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-UNPACK-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 7, 8.  
**Customer phrasing:** Remove tape residue safely; How to move refrigerator without scratching floor?.  

**Unpacking:** Remove tape/glue using a small amount of liquid dish soap and warm water, then dry. Avoid abrasive products, alcohol and flammable cleaners. Remove/recycle packaging and clean before use.

**Moving:** Cover floors with hardboard or cardboard, pull the appliance straight out and do not “walk” or wiggle it, which may damage flooring. Measure the intended doorway before deciding whether any door removal is needed.

**Door-removal prerequisite:** For a previously used installation, turn controls OFF, unplug, remove food and loose door bins, and use supported lifting arrangements.

### KA-WATER-PRESSURE-001 — Household water supply, pressure and RO considerations

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-WATER-PRESSURE-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 12.  
**Customer phrasing:** Required water pressure; Why are ice cubes hollow?; Reverse osmosis with KitchenAid fridge.  

**Required supply:** A cold-water connection at **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)** is required for the dispenser and ice maker. The manual notes lower pressure can reduce water flow and create hollow or irregular ice cubes.

**Reverse osmosis:** Check a blocked sediment filter and allow the RO storage tank to refill after heavy use. For water-pressure uncertainty, contact a licensed qualified plumber; do not assume a second filter is harmless to flow.

### KA-DOOR-REMOVAL-001 — Refrigerator-door removal and reattachment

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-DOOR-REMOVAL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 8, 9, 10.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to remove left door with water dispenser?; Which hinge has water tubing?; Reattach refrigerator doors.  

**Use case:** If the cabinet cannot pass through a doorway, the manual shows right- and left-hand door removal/reinstallation as separate sequences. Required tools include a **3/16-inch hex-key wrench** and **#2 Phillips screwdriver**. Disconnect power, keep doors supported and do not rely on magnetic gaskets to hold a door while hinges are loose.

**Critical difference:** Water-dispenser tubing and wiring pass through the **left-hand hinge** and must be disconnected before removing that door. The manual distinguishes different tubing-connector styles and warns **not to disconnect the green ground wire**. During reassembly, keep the tubing and wiring bundles separate and reconnect correctly. Do not fully tighten hinge screws before alignment is complete.

### KA-FREEZER-DRAWER-001 — Freezer drawer front removal and reinstallation

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-FREEZER-DRAWER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 11.  
**Customer phrasing:** Remove freezer drawer front; Plastic studs on freezer glide.  

**Prerequisites:** Disconnect refrigerator power and remove frozen food before moving; two people may be required to handle the drawer front. The required tool is a **1/4-inch hex driver**.

**Removal sequence:** Fully open the freezer drawer; remove the two inside upper screws securing the front to glides; lift the front to release plastic studs from glide-bracket slots. Reinstall by aligning the studs and replacing the two screws as illustrated, then reconnect power only after completing the installation steps.

**Original PDF visual reference:** See the **unlabeled two-sided drawer-front screw-and-plastic-stud diagrams** under “Remove and Replace Freezer Drawer Front” on printed page 11.

### KA-LEVEL-001 — Leveling, closing and aligning doors

**Knowledge ID:** `KA-LEVEL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator models to which guide W11218735B applies; features marked “on some models” require inspection of fitted options  
**Source:** French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator — Use & Care Guide, PDF page(s) 15, 16.  
**Customer phrasing:** Doors not closing by themselves; Uneven door tops; How to use brake feet.  

**When doors do not close or line up:** Adjust cabinet tilt/leveling and check door alignment, not the temperature dial. The guide describes the **brake feet** and bottom-hinge alignment screw.

**Cabinet tilt:** With the freezer drawer open, turn both brake feet **clockwise by the same amount** using a **1/4-inch hex driver** to raise the front and make doors close more readily.

**Door alignment:** The right refrigerator door has an adjustment inside the bottom hinge pin, operated with the short end of the **1/8-inch hex key** shown. Check the two refrigerator doors at their top edges and clearance to the freezer drawer after adjustment.

**Original PDF visual reference:** The **unlabeled leveling-foot and right-bottom-hinge illustrations** under “Refrigerator Leveling, Door Closing and Alignment” on printed pages 15–16 show the adjustment points.

## Technician safety and service authorization boundaries

**Original manual:** *Fast Track Troubleshooting*. **Source file:** `repair-guide.pdf`.

### SFT-SAFETY-001 — Technician-only safety boundary and support

**Knowledge ID:** `SFT-SAFETY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381 and RF28K9380 as printed on this service sheet; individual procedures may be feature-specific  
**Source:** Fast Track Troubleshooting, PDF page(s) 1.  
**Customer phrasing:** Is this service guide intended for homeowners?; Where is technician support?.  

**What it means:** This fast-track sheet is marked **For Technicians Only**. It assumes electrical, electronic and mechanical competence at the appliance-repair-trade level. The sheet warns that attempted major-appliance repair can cause injury or property damage. Customer-support agents should not convert board-voltage or refrigerant procedures into casual do-it-yourself advice.

**Technical support printed in the sheet:** ASC/SSD 1-888-751-4086; FE/ME 1-866-894-0637; GSPN `http://gspn3.samsungcsportal.com/main.jsp`; E-learning `samsungasc.earlyconnect.com`; MCP/E-learning support 1-800-749-9421, `e-learning@sea.samsung.com`.

**Service escalation:** For electrical measurements, board repair or mechanical extraction, refer the customer to personnel with the experience specified in the sheet.

### UNKNOWN-SFT-WARRANTY — Warranty and consumer-level repair authorizations not established in fast-track sheet

**Knowledge ID:** `UNKNOWN-SFT-WARRANTY`  
**Evidence:** [UNKNOWN]  
**Applies to:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381 and RF28K9380 as printed on this service sheet; individual procedures may be feature-specific  
**Source:** Fast Track Troubleshooting, PDF page(s) 1, 11.  
**Customer phrasing:** Is inverter replacement covered under warranty?; Can I perform circuit board testing myself?.  

**What IS documented:** Technician-only service procedures, limited service-support contact information and high-voltage cautions appear in the sheet.

**What is NOT established:** The sheet does not give the customer's model-specific warranty term, a complete parts-interchangeability matrix or permission for a homeowner to perform live electrical measurements.

**Safe response:** Ask for the exact appliance model and an applicable consumer warranty or service document; for hazardous service steps refer to appropriately qualified technicians.

**Related documented units:** `SFT-SAFETY-001`, `SFT-ELECTRONICS-001`.

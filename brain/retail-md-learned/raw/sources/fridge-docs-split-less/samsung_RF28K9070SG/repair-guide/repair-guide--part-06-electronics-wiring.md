### SFT-TDM-VALVE-001 — Natural defrost and four-way TDM refrigerant valve

**Knowledge ID:** `SFT-TDM-VALVE-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381 and RF28K9380 as printed on this service sheet; individual procedures may be feature-specific  
**Source:** Fast Track Troubleshooting, PDF page(s) 10.  
**Customer phrasing:** What is a TDM four way valve?; FF yellow tube FRE no color; Natural defrosting.  

**Natural defrosting:** The page describes raising refrigerator humidity by running the fan when cooling is not active to remove frost from the evaporator.

**TDM four-way valve:** The source defines “swing operation” as movement of a **PAD** inside the four-way valve to route refrigerant to different evaporators. The parallel-cycle schematic is labelled as applying to **one compressor**.

**Tube markings in the printed valve photo:** `#1` input from drier; `#3` outputs to evaporators; FF section **yellow tube**; FRE section **no color**; convertible zone **red tube**.

**Original PDF visual reference:** See the **unlabeled valve schematic, parallel-cycle diagram and valve photograph** under “TDM 4 Way Valve,” “About the Parallel Cycle” and “TDM VALVE” on printed page 10 of *Fast Track Troubleshooting*. These are physical identification references, not a standalone coolant-servicing procedure.

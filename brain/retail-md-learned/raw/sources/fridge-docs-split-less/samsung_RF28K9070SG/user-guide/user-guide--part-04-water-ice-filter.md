## Water and ice operation, ice bucket and filter procedures

**Original manual:** *Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance)*. **Source file:** `user-guide.pdf`.

### SAM-DISPENSER-001 — Water and ice dispenser order and spill prevention

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-DISPENSER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 50.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to get ice and water together?; Water smells after several unused days; Why does one lever not work when both pressed?.  

**Use:** Select chilled water/cubed ice/crushed ice as appropriate. For combined ice and water, place the glass under the **ice lever (A)** and press, then under **water lever (B)** and press. If both are pressed at once, only the first lever operates.

**Avoid spills:** Wait **1 second** after dispensing before removing the glass; position it centrally. If water has not been dispensed for **2–3 days**, the first **1–2 glasses** may have abnormal taste or odor; the manual says to discard those initial glasses and does not call that alone a failure.

**Original PDF visual reference:** The **unlabeled dispenser-lever diagram** under “Water dispenser” on printed page 50 identifies levers A and B.

### SAM-FILTER-001 — Water-filter replacement and reset steps

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-FILTER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 59, 60, 61.  
**Customer phrasing:** Replace Samsung water filter; Filter indicator stays on; Water spurts after replacing filter.  

**Approved filter rule:** Use only Samsung-provided or approved replacement filters. The guide cautions against third-party products.

**Documented sequence:** Unlock the old cartridge by turning its knob **90° counterclockwise**; pull it straight out, keeping it aligned to avoid leaks and emptying the tray if necessary. Insert the replacement and turn clockwise to lock. Hold **Crushed Ice for 3 seconds** to reset the filter-life detector. Open the water valve and run water through the dispenser for approximately **7 minutes** to purge air/impurities.

**Notes:** Air introduced during filter change may cause brief spurting or dripping; wipe escaped water and monitor for persistent leakage. Resetting the indicator without replacing the filter is not equivalent to renewed filtration.

### SAM-ICE-BUCKET-001 — Removing and reseating the ice bucket

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-ICE-BUCKET-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 52.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to remove Samsung ice bucket?; Ice bucket will not slide in; Ice cubes are clumping.  

**Documented steps:** Hold the bottom and side of the bucket; gently lift and pull it out. Remove remaining cubes and reinstall. If the bucket cannot seat firmly, the manual says to turn the **ice screw 90 degrees** and try again. When turning Ice Maker Off, remove old ice so it does not clump.

**Original PDF visual reference:** See the **unlabeled ice-bucket removal illustrations** under “To remove the ice bucket” on printed page 52 of *Refrigerator — User manual*. The manual notes that temporary removal does not itself affect refrigerator thermal or mechanical performance.

### SAM-ICE-MAKING-001 — Initial ice production and safe ice maker self-test

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-ICE-MAKING-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 51, 52.  
**Customer phrasing:** Ice maker takes too long; Ice maker test button chime; Why is ice bucket jammed?.  

**First production:** After installation, allow at least **24 hours**. Dispense the first **4–6 cubes**, wait **8 hours** and dispense another 4–6, then wait **16 hours** before dispensing the first full glass. Using all ice at once can require an additional wait of **8 hours** for initial cubes.

**Maker check:** The manual identifies a **Test** button on the side of the ice maker. Pressing it sounds a chime, followed by another chime if operation is normal. Repeated testing when the tray is full risks overflow/jams, so it says **do not repeatedly press Test**. Repeated test chimes warrant a waterline installer or Samsung service contact.

**Safety:** Keep food, fingers and objects out of the ice maker/dispense outlet. After loss of power, empty melted/re-frozen ice in the bucket before normal use.

### SAM-ICE-PANEL-001 — Ice Maker Off, Cubed Ice and filter reset

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-ICE-PANEL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 40, 41.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to select crushed ice?; Reset the water filter light; Turn off the ice maker.  

**Ice Maker Off:** The corresponding panel control enables or disables ice production; turning it off does not necessarily remove ice already in the bucket. Consult the bucket instructions before lengthy shutdown.

**Ice selection:** Select **Cubed Ice** or **Crushed Ice** on the feature panel before dispensing.

**Filter reset:** Following cartridge replacement, hold **Crushed Ice for 3 seconds** to reset the filter-life indicator; the filter indicator should go off. This is a reminder reset, not evidence that water has been filtered or a leak repaired.

### SAM-RO-WATER-001 — Reverse-osmosis water-pressure conditions and filter ordering

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-RO-WATER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 62.  
**Customer phrasing:** Can I use reverse osmosis?; Water pressure with RO low; Where to buy Samsung water filter?.  

**Documented reverse-osmosis requirements:** The supply to the reverse-osmosis system must be **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)**, and the filtered line feeding the refrigerator must be **at least 40 psi (276 kPa)**. If below those requirements, check for a blocked RO sediment filter, refill the RO tank and consider the pressure loss introduced by a refrigerator filter. The manual directs further water-pressure questions to licensed plumbing professionals.

**Replacement services:** The manual lists Samsung service centers and `samsungparts.com` for filters, and recommends service center replacement of refrigerator LED lamps.

### SAM-WATER-CLOUDING-001 — Temporary cloudiness in dispensed water

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-WATER-CLOUDING-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 53.  
**Customer phrasing:** Cloudy water from dispenser; Why is filtered water milky?.  

**Normal explanation:** The manual explains that its filtering process can temporarily leave water saturated with oxygen and nitrogen, creating a cloudy appearance which should clear **after a few seconds**. That described temporary cloudiness by itself is not labeled a failure; do not automatically infer microbial safety or a defective filter from appearance alone.

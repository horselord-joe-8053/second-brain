### SAM-INSTALL-LOCATION-001 — Installation site, clearances, dimensions and flooring

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-INSTALL-LOCATION-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]

**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 27, 28, 29.  
**Customer phrasing:** How much space does this fridge need?; What are Samsung fridge dimensions?; Recommended installation temperature.  

**Site requirements:** Use a solid level surface without carpet or flooring obstructing ventilation; avoid direct sun and heat sources; provide door-opening and service space. The permitted installation location temperature printed here is **50–110°F (10–43°C)**.

**Dimensions in the illustrated installation chart:** Depth A **34 1/4 in (871 mm)**, width B **35 3/4 in (908 mm)**, height C **70 3/4 in (1797 mm)**, overall height D **71 7/8 in (1825 mm)**. The page-28 clearance illustration includes a **2 in (50 mm)** clearance callout and **125°** opening depiction, with further dimensions tied to numbered arrows; do not treat these as interchangeable measures.

**Floor protection:** The floor must carry the refrigerator at full load. Use cardboard beneath the legs when moving for installation and protect its travel path if later repositioning.

### SAM-INSTALL-LEVEL-001 — Leveling feet and door alignment

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-INSTALL-LEVEL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 30, 31, 32.  
**Customer phrasing:** Refrigerator leaning forward; How to adjust the door gap?; Which way to turn the leveling feet?.  

**Leveling:** With the refrigerator empty, use the front-leg levellers on a solid, flat floor. The source states the **front should be a little higher than the rear**. Turning the leveller **clockwise raises** and counterclockwise lowers the associated side.

**Door height:** The supplied snap rings come in **1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5 mm** sizes. Raise and hold the lower door, and insert one suitable snap ring between the door and hinge. The manual warns **never stack two or more rings**, since they can slip or rub.

**Door gap on applicable RF28K907* models only:** After removing the relevant top cover, use a **4 mm Allen wrench** on the gap bolt; **clockwise widens** and **counterclockwise narrows** the gap. Reinstall the cover when done.

**Original PDF visual reference:** The **unlabeled leveller, snap-ring and door-gap illustrations** under Steps 3–4 on printed pages 30–32 show the actual adjusting points.

### SAM-WATER-INSTALL-001 — Water-line installation, pressure and initial flushing

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-WATER-INSTALL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** the Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations and optional features illustrated in this edition; this document does not print an exhaustive model-suffix compatibility list on the cover  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 33, 34, 35.  
**Customer phrasing:** What water pressure is needed for ice?; Flush water line after installation; Water line connection is leaking.  

**Water pressure:** The refrigerator ice-maker connection requires **20–125 psi**; below **20 psi**, the manual mentions a booster pump as a possible installation measure. The refrigerator water filter removes particles but **does not sterilize or destroy microorganisms**.

**Connection:** Use a new hose set and the specified quarter-inch fittings; the illustrated parts distinguish copper tubing with compression nut/ferrules and plastic tubing with a molded bulb end. Do not use plastic tubing without that molded end. Tighten but do not overtighten the compression nut, turn on water and inspect for leaks; if leaks appear, turn water off and correct the connection.

**Start-up:** Flush **1 gallon** of water through the filter, about **6 minutes**. Let the ice maker produce ice for **1–2 days** and discard the first **1–2 buckets**. After connecting the line, dispense water until the internal reservoir is filled.

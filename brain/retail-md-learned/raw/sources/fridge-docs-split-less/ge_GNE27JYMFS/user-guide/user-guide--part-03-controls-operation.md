### GE-KNOB-CONTROL-001 — Internal rotary control for GBE21D and GBE21A

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-KNOB-CONTROL-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GBE21D / GBE21A rotary-control models only  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 8.  
**Customer phrasing:** How to set rotary fridge temperature?; GBE21D knob position; Why does setting take 24 hours?.  

**Applies to:** **GBE21D and GBE21A** rotary/internal-control variants shown beside their controls; other control types use different procedures.

**Documented behavior:** Refrigerator/freezer settings are adjusted at dedicated internal controls. The printed knob-position guide establishes the relative positions. **Electrical precaution:** Power cannot be disconnected by any setting on the control panel.

### GE-NORMAL-SOUNDS-001 — Normal humming, whooshing, fans, defrost and gurgling

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-NORMAL-SOUNDS-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 44.  
**Customer phrasing:** Is humming normal?; Why is fridge making whooshing sound?; Fan noise changes speed.  

**Normal conditions explained:** The manual says a newer high-efficiency compressor may run longer or faster with high-pitched hum/pulsation; closing doors may make a **whooshing** sound as pressure equalizes. Fans change speed when first plugged in, after frequent openings and after food is added, to maintain temperatures.

**Customer route:** Match the sound to normal-state examples before recommending service. The manufacturer also describes various clicks, cracks and liquid movement during cooling/defrost on this page. However, an unusual noise cannot be diagnosed as normal without checking its full behavior.

**Customer phrasing:** How to clean fingerprint-resistant GE steel?; How to move refrigerator out from wall?; Refrigerator LED light not working.  

**Clean safely:** The manual distinguishes finish-specific exterior cleaning and interior care; avoid cleaning cold glass with hot water and remove spills/food odors routinely. Check the finish (fingerprint-resistant stainless, black stainless, slate, dark slate, etc.) before choosing a cleaner. Do not unplug by pulling on the cord.

**Behind the refrigerator:** Pull straight out when moving; protect flooring and avoid rolling over water lines. The guide discusses preparing for vacations/power interruptions and replacing/servicing LEDs by fitted type.

**Model boundary:** Lighting location and replacement instructions depend on model; do not assume every LED bulb is a customer-replaceable lamp.

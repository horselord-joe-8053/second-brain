### GE-WATER-LINE-001 — Water installation, tubing approvals, RO and shutoff valve

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-WATER-LINE-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 41, 42, 43.  
**Customer phrasing:** Required GE water pressure; GE RO filter bypass; Which waterline tubing is approved?.  

**Supply:** A cold household water line at **25–120 psi (172–827 kPa)** is required. The GE guide names copper kits **WX8X2, WX8X3 and WX8X4** and approved plastic Universal Water Line Refrigerator Tubing part numbers **WX08X10006, WX08X10015 and WX08X10025**.

**Reverse osmosis:** For GE RO systems, the guide specifies a **GE Appliances RVKit**; for other RO systems, follow the manufacturer's recommendation. If using RO with a refrigerator water filter, the GE manual says to use a **filter bypass plug** in relevant configurations to avoid hollow ice cubes.

**Installation:** It covers water shutoff, tubing, valves, sleeves and compression fittings. The page-42 safety note warns unapproved plastic tubing may crack or rupture under pressure. On page 43 the guide states that saddle valves are illegal in Massachusetts under the cited code and customers should consult a licensed plumber.

**Original PDF visual reference:** The **unlabeled tubing, valve-clamp and connection drawings** under “INSTALLING THE WATER LINE” on printed pages 41–43 identify parts and order.

**Customer phrasing:** How long is GE refrigerator warranty?; GE Profile five year sealed system; Water filter 30-day limited warranty.  

**Printed U.S. warranty:** A **one-year** limited coverage period on parts that fail due to defects in materials/workmanship, with labor/related service as specified. **Service:** Original proof of purchase and the source's specified service rules apply. Do not extend the five-year GE Profile/Café sealed-system terms automatically to standard GE variants.

**Limit:** This is the printed version of the warranty and not a real-time determination of eligibility.

### GE-XWFE-WARRANTY-001 — XWFE cartridge-only replacement warranty

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-XWFE-WARRANTY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 49.  
**Customer phrasing:** GE XWFE filter cartridge warranty; Does filter warranty cover labor?.  

**Separate warranty:** This page sets out a water-filter cartridge/part warranty, requiring purchase proof for a defect claim made within **30 days of original purchase** under its stated conditions. Abuse, improper installation and labor/removal/diagnosis costs are excluded as printed.

**Customer route:** Distinguish this replaceable filter warranty from the refrigerator appliance warranty; do not promise labor coverage for cartridge installation.

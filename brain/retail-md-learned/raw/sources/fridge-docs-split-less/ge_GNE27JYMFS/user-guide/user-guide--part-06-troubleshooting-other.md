### GE-SYMPTOM-032 — Water is leaking from dispenser

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-032`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately

**Customer phrasing:** Water is leaking from dispenser; GE refrigerator water is leaking from dispenser.  

**Customer symptom:** Water is leaking from dispenser.

**Documented cause/check pairs from the manual:**
- **Possible cause:** Glass not being held under the dispenser long enough after button is released.  **Documented check/action:** Hold the glass underneath the dispenser for 2–3 seconds after releasing the dispenser button. Water may continue to dispense after the button is released
- **Possible cause:** Air may be present in the water line system, causing water to drip after being dispensed.  **Documented check/action:** Dispense water for at least 2 minutes to remove air from system.

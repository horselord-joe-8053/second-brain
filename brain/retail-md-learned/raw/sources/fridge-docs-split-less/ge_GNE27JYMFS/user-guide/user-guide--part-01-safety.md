### GE-SAFETY-001 — Safety, electrical grounding and child-entrapment warnings

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SAFETY-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 3, 4.  
**Customer phrasing:** Refrigerator grounded outlet requirement; Can cold glass shelves be cleaned with hot water?; Child safety and disposal.  

**Core safety restrictions:** Keep fingers clear of door pinch points; avoid touching freezer surfaces with wet hands; do not expose cold glass shelves to warm water or impact; do not refreeze completely thawed food. Do not put hands near the icemaker moving parts or ice crushing opening.

**Grounding:** The refrigerator has a **grounded three-prong plug**. The guide prohibits removal of the ground prong, adapter plugs and extension cords; if only a two-prong outlet is present, have a qualified electrician supply a properly grounded three-prong outlet. Use a dedicated, properly rated electrical supply.

**Old refrigerator disposal:** Follow the documented door/entrapment and disposal precautions; keep children out of abandoned appliances.

**Customer-facing warning:** A change on the temperature control panel does **not** disconnect power to the unit. Physically disconnect electricity before service as the manual requires.

### GE-R600A-001 — Flammable R600a refrigerant safety and authorized service

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-R600A-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 5.  
**Customer phrasing:** Is my GE fridge using flammable refrigerant?; Refrigerant leak safety; Who can service R600a?.  

**Model-specific restriction:** Check the refrigerator's rating label for refrigerant type. If it identifies **R600a/isobutane**, the manual warns of a **fire or explosion hazard**. Avoid damaging refrigeration tubing; repair is to be performed by authorized service personnel using manufacturer-authorized replacement parts. Disposal follows federal/local regulations.

**Safe response:** Do not suggest cutting, heating, brazing or puncturing the sealed system. Do not assume every model covered by this booklet uses R600a.

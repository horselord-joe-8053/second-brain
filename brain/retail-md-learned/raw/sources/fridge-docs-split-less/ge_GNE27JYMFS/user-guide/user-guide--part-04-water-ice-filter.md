### GE-DISPENSER-001 — External and internal dispenser operation, initial purge

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-DISPENSER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 13.  
**Customer phrasing:** Water not dispensing after installation; How to purge water line?; Is dispenser spill tray draining?.  

**Water/ice dispenser:** Press a glass gently against the cradle. The spill shelf is **not self-draining**; it needs regular cleaning. If no water appears after first installation, trapped air may need purging: press the dispenser paddle for **at least five minutes** to remove trapped air and fill the system, then discard the **first six full glasses** to flush impurities.

**Safety:** Never put fingers or objects into the ice-crusher discharge opening; use a sturdy glass to avoid breakage. The internal water-dispenser variant is described separately on the same printed page.

### GE-ICEMAKER-001 — Automatic ice maker operation and freezer variant

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-ICEMAKER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 20, 21.  
**Customer phrasing:** When does GE start making ice?; How many cubes per day?; Where is the ice maker switch?.  

**Initial operation:** The manual warns that a newly installed refrigerator may take **12–24 hours** before making ice. A printed estimate is **ten cubes per cycle** and about **100–130 cubes in 24 hours**, subject to actual model and operating conditions.

**Icemaker location:** The manual covers icemakers positioned in the refrigerator door and in the freezer compartment on different fitted versions. Check which is present before describing switch or bin location.

**Customer route:** Verify icemaker enabled, water connection, freezer temperature, door closure and clear bin without placing hands in moving parts.

### GE-XWFE-FILTER-001 — XWFE water filter location, RFID and replacement

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-XWFE-FILTER-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 14, 15.  
**Customer phrasing:** Replace XWFE filter; Where is GE filter?; Filter red light blinking; GE filter RFID.  

**Location:** XWFE cartridge holder is in the **upper-right rear** of the refrigerator compartment on fitted variants.

**Variant matters:** Some models use RFID to monitor filter status and detect leakage. The manual explains filter-life indicators and replacement, plus a **filter bypass plug** for applicable models when not using the cartridge. Do not assume that every model detects the cartridge electronically.

**Customer procedure:** Locate and remove the cartridge using the illustrated locking method, fit the correct replacement/bypass plug, reseat and flush according to the installation directions, then reset the replacement indicator using the panel appropriate to the model. Do not invent third-party compatibility.

### GE-XWFE-PERFORMANCE-001 — XWFE certification, capacity and supply operating limits

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-XWFE-PERFORMANCE-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9; specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately  
**Source:** Refrigerators — Bottom Freezer: Owner’s Manual & Installation Instructions, PDF page(s) 50.  
**Customer phrasing:** How many gallons is XWFE rated for?; GE filter NSF P473; XWFE flow rate.  

**Performance context:** The GE XWFE performance data sheet cites certification by **IAPMO R&T** against **NSF/ANSI 42, 53, 401 and P473** for the specified contaminant claims; actual reductions depend on local water quality and adherence to installation and replacement instructions.

**Application values printed here:** Service flow **0.5 gpm (1.89 L/min)**, potable supply water, pressure **25–120 psi (172–827 kPa)**, water temperature **33–100°F (0.6–38°C)**, capacity **170 gallons (643.5 liters)**.

**Customer route:** The capacity applies to this XWFE system, not to all refrigerator filters. Do not claim that the filter makes microbiologically unsafe water safe.

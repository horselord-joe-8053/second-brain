# FRENCH DOOR BOTTOM MOUNT REFRIGERATOR — USE & CARE GUIDE

This guide provides comprehensive operational and safety instructions for the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS French-door bottom mount refrigerator. It covers installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and safety guidelines essential for optimal performance.

## Safety Guidelines
- Connect only to a properly grounded three-prong electrical supply.
- Use at least two people to move/install the refrigerator.
- Do not use unauthorized electrical devices inside storage compartments.
- Disconnect power before service and cleaning.

## Parts and Operating Characteristics
- High-efficiency variable-speed compressor.
- Optional dual evaporators for improved climate control.
- NSF-certified water filter for safe drinking water.

## Refrigeration Temperature Controls
- Recommended settings: Fridge at 37°F (3°C) and Freezer at 0°F (−18°C).
- Adjustments should be made gradually, waiting 24 hours between changes.

## Extra Modes, Alarms, and Display Messages
- **Max Cool**: Activates for 24 hours to cool down after loading groceries.
- **Sabbath Mode**: Disables lights and alarms for religious observance.
- **Vacation Mode**: Reduces energy consumption during extended absences.

## Water Supply Installation
- Household potable cold supply should deliver 35–120 psi.
- Installation should be performed by a qualified technician.

## Water Filter Replacement
- Replace the water filter every 6 months.
- Follow specific installation and flushing instructions after replacement.

## Troubleshooting
| Customer complaint / synonyms | Source-supported questions and actions |
|---|---|
| Fridge won't operate, screen off | Confirm power cord into grounded supply, outlet, fuse/breaker, cooling set ON and showroom mode OFF. |
| Temperature too warm | Check control set points and ensure doors/vents are not blocked. |
| Ice tastes/smells bad | Discard old ice and flush the system after filter changes. |

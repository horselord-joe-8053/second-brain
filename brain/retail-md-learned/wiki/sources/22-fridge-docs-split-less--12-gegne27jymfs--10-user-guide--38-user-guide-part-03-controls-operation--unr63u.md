# Controls, Operation, Features, Storage and Care

This document provides detailed operational instructions for various GE refrigerator models, focusing on control types, temperature settings, and maintenance. It covers specific models and their respective control mechanisms, including rotary, top, and external controls.

## Rotary Control

### Overview
- **Models:** GBE21D / GBE21A
- **Functionality:** Adjusts refrigerator/freezer settings at dedicated internal controls.
- **Stabilization Time:** 24 hours for temperatures to stabilize after adjustment.

### Instructions
1. Set the knob to the desired position.
2. Wait for 24 hours to allow the temperature to stabilize.

### Electrical Precaution
- Power cannot be disconnected by any setting on the control panel.

### Visual Reference
- Refer to **Figure B** on printed page 8 of the owner's manual for knob-position guidance.

## Top Control

### Overview
- **Models:** GNE27J / GNE27E and others in the GDE and GWE families.
- **Functionality:** Control panel located at the top for temperature adjustments.

### Instructions
1. Adjust the freezer compartment temperature to 0°F (−17.8°C) and the refrigerator to 37°F (2.8°C).
2. Allow 24 hours for stabilization.

### Features
- **TurboCool™ Mode:** Activated by pressing the designated control for three seconds.
- **Filter Status Display:** Shows remaining filter life percentage.

### Visual Reference
- Refer to **Figure B** on printed page 9 of the owner's manual for control layout.

## External Control

### Overview
- **Models:** GYE18J, GYE21J, PYE18H, GFE24J, PFE24H, GFE26J
- **Functionality:** Control panel located on the exterior for user adjustments.

### Instructions
1. Set the freezer to 0°F (−17.8°C) and the refrigerator to 37°F (2.8°C).
2. Allow 24 hours for stabilization.
3. Use the filter reset button as indicated on the panel.

### Visual Reference
- Refer to **Figure C** on printed pages 10-11 of the owner's manual for external control layout.

## Sabbath Mode

### Overview
- **Functionality:** Alters the refrigerator's operation to comply with Sabbath observance.

### Instructions
1. Activate Sabbath Mode to turn off temperature control and advanced features (e.g., door alarm).
2. Depending on the model, lights may remain off or dim regardless of door activity.
3. The compressor will run on a timed defrost pattern while in Sabbath mode.

### Visual Reference

## Normal Sounds

### Overview
- **Functionality:** Understanding typical operational noises made by the refrigerator.

### Common Sounds
- **Humming:** Normal for high-efficiency compressors.
- **Whooshing:** Occurs when doors close as pressure equalizes.
- **Fan Noise:** Changes speed based on usage and temperature maintenance.

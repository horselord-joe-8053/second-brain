## Warranty Overview

This document provides warranty and support information for GE Appliances refrigerators, specifically focusing on the limited warranty, water filter warranty, and consumer support options.

### Limited Warranty

- **Coverage**: One-year limited warranty on parts that fail due to defects in materials or workmanship.
- **Exclusions**: Labor and related service as specified.
- **Extended Coverage**: Five-year sealed system coverage for GE Profile™ and GE Café™ models under specific conditions.

### Water Filter Warranty

- **XWFE Filter Cartridge**: 
  - **Coverage**: 30-day warranty on the water filter cartridge.
  - **Exclusions**: Labor for installation is not covered.

### Consumer Support

- **Support Options**: 
  - Online support and appliance registration.
  - Authorized service scheduling.
  - Contact information for customer support:
    - U.S. support number: **833.4BODEWELL**
    - Canadian scheduling number: **800.561.3344**

### Additional Information

- **Proof of Purchase**: Required for warranty claims.
- **Model Verification**: Specific model families and fitted controls must be checked separately.

### Regulatory Compliance

- **Wi-Fi Capabilities**: Some models may have optional connectivity features, subject to FCC Part 15 compliance.

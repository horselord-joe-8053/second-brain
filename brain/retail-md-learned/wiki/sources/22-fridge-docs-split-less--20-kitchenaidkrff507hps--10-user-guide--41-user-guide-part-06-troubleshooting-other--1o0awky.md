# KitchenAid KRFF507HPS User Guide: Troubleshooting Other Issues

This document provides troubleshooting steps for various symptoms related to the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator. It details potential causes and solutions for common issues users may encounter.

## Troubleshooting Symptoms

### KA-SYMPTOM-018 — Clatter
- **Customer symptom:** Clatter.
- **Possible causes:** May be heard when the ice falls into the ice storage bin.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** This is normal.

### KA-SYMPTOM-019 — Temperature is too warm
- **Customer symptom:** Temperature is too warm.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator has just been installed.
  - Cooling is turned off.
  - The controls are not set correctly for the surrounding conditions.
  - The door(s) are opened often or not closed completely.
  - A large load of food was recently added.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Allow 24 hours following installation for the refrigerator to cool completely.
  - Turn on cooling.
  - Adjust the controls a setting colder.
  - Minimize door openings and keep doors fully closed.

### KA-SYMPTOM-020 — Temperature is too cold in the refrigerator/crisper
- **Customer symptom:** Temperature is too cold in the refrigerator/crisper.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator air vent(s) are blocked.
  - The controls are not set correctly for the surrounding conditions.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Move items away from the air vent.
  - Adjust the controls a setting warmer.

### KA-SYMPTOM-021 — There is interior moisture buildup
- **Customer symptom:** There is interior moisture buildup.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The room is humid.
  - The door(s) are opened often or not closed completely.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Minimize door openings and close doors completely.
  - Keep all containers tightly covered.

### KA-SYMPTOM-022 — There is frost/ice buildup in the freezer compartment
- **Customer symptom:** There is frost/ice buildup in the freezer compartment.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The drawer is opened often or left open.
  - Poor door seal.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Minimize drawer openings and close drawer completely after use.

### KA-SYMPTOM-023 — The ice maker is not producing ice or is not producing enough ice
- **Customer symptom:** The ice maker is not producing ice or is not producing enough ice.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator is not connected to a water supply.
  - The ice maker is not turned on.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Connect refrigerator to water supply and turn water shutoff valve fully open.
  - Wait 24 hours after ice maker installation for ice production to begin.

### KA-SYMPTOM-024 — The ice cubes are hollow or small
- **Customer symptom:** The ice cubes are hollow or small.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The water shutoff valve is not fully open.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Turn the water shutoff valve fully open.

### KA-SYMPTOM-025 — Off-taste, odor or gray color in the ice or water
- **Customer symptom:** Off-taste, odor or gray color in the ice or water.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - Newly installed refrigerator requires flush of water system.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Flush the water system and discard the first 3 batches of ice produced.

### KA-SYMPTOM-026 — The water and ice dispenser will not operate properly
- **Customer symptom:** The water and ice dispenser will not operate properly.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator is not connected to a water supply.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Connect the refrigerator to the water supply and turn the water shutoff valve fully open.

### KA-SYMPTOM-027 — The ice dispenser jams while dispensing crushed ice
- **Customer symptom:** The ice dispenser jams while dispensing crushed ice.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - Crushed ice is blocking the ice delivery chute.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Switch from crushed ice to cubed ice to clear the ice dispenser.

### KA-SYMPTOM-028 — Water is leaking or dripping from the dispenser system
- **Customer symptom:** Water is leaking or dripping from the dispenser system.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The glass was not held under the dispenser long enough.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Hold the glass under the dispenser 2 to 3 seconds after releasing the dispenser lever.

### KA-SYMPTOM-029 — Water continues to drip from the dispenser beyond the first few drops
- **Customer symptom:** Water continues to drip from the dispenser beyond the first few drops.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator has just been installed.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Flushing the water system removes the air from the lines which causes the water dispenser to drip.

### KA-SYMPTOM-030 — Water from the dispenser is warm
- **Customer symptom:** Water from the dispenser is warm.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator has just been installed.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Allow 24 hours after installation for the water supply to cool completely.

### KA-SYMPTOM-031 — The doors will not close completely
- **Customer symptom:** The doors will not close completely.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The door is blocked open.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Move food packages away from door.

### KA-SYMPTOM-032 — The doors are difficult to open
- **Customer symptom:** The doors are difficult to open.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The door gaskets are dirty or sticky.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Clean gaskets and contact surfaces with mild soap and warm water.

### KA-SYMPTOM-033 — The doors appear to be uneven
- **Customer symptom:** The doors appear to be uneven.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The doors need to be aligned.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - See “Refrigerator Leveling, Door Closing and Alignment.”

### KA-SYMPTOM-034 — The refrigerator is unsteady or it rolls forward when opening and closing doors
- **Customer symptom:** The refrigerator is unsteady or it rolls forward when opening and closing doors.
- **Possible causes:** 
  - The refrigerator brake feet are not snug against the floor.
- **Documented checks / solutions:** 
  - Remove the base grille and turn both brake feet clockwise until they are snug against the floor.

# Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance)

This user manual provides essential safety guidelines for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, focusing on installation, usage, and disposal. It emphasizes the importance of following safety precautions to prevent accidents and ensure proper operation.

## Safety Precautions

- Users must adhere to specific safety precautions to prevent accidents, such as ensuring proper grounding and avoiding flammable materials near the appliance.
- The manual emphasizes that only qualified technicians should perform internal repairs to avoid safety hazards.
- Proper disposal methods are critical to prevent environmental harm and ensure safety, particularly concerning refrigerants.

## Grounding and Electrical Safety

- Instructions regarding proper grounding and electrical connections to prevent electric shock or fire hazards.
- Ensures that users understand the importance of electrical safety in relation to the refrigerator.

## Disposal Guidelines

- Instructions on how to safely dispose of the refrigerator, including handling refrigerants and seals.
- Addresses environmental concerns and safety during the disposal process.

## Important Safety Messages

- Read the manual's WARNING/CAUTION messages before installation and use.
- Important stated hazards include improper earthing or damaged plugs, children trapped in discarded refrigerators, fingers or objects in dispenser/ice-maker outlets, blocked ventilation, pressure on glass shelves, spraying water near electrical parts, and inflammable materials near the appliance.
- The manual requires qualified technicians for servicing.

## Customer-safe Priorities

- Keep ventilation openings clear.
- Do not use the refrigerator with a damaged power cord or plug.
- Disconnect the plug before servicing.
- Avoid using benzene, thinner, or highly flammable cleaning agents.
- Use the appliance only for its intended household functions.

## Disposal Warnings

- Follow the manual's critical disposal warnings, including handling doors/seals and refrigerant or insulating materials as specified.
- Check local disposal requirements rather than improvising.

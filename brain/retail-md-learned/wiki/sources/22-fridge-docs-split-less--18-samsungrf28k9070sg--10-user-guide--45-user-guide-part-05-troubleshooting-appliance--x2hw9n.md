## Troubleshooting Procedures

This document serves as a troubleshooting guide for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, detailing common issues and their resolutions. The following sections outline specific customer symptoms, documented checks, and actions to address each issue.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-001 — The refrigerator does not operate or cool

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-001`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations.  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 63.  

**Customer phrasing:** The refrigerator does not operate or cool; How to fix the refrigerator does not operate or cool.  

**Documented checks and actions:**
1. Ensure the power cord is connected properly.
2. Check that the temperature control is set correctly; try setting to a lower temperature.
3. Ensure the refrigerator is not exposed to direct sunlight or near a heat source.
4. Verify that the refrigerator is positioned far enough from walls or cabinets for proper ventilation.
5. Avoid overloading the refrigerator, as too much food can block internal ventilation.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-002 — Food in the fridge freezes

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-002`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations.  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 63.  

**Customer phrasing:** Food in the fridge freezes; How to fix food in the fridge freezes.  

**Documented checks and actions:**
1. Check if the temperature control is set to the lowest temperature; if so, change to a higher setting.
2. Consider the ambient room temperature; if it's too low, adjust accordingly.
3. Ensure that food with high water content is not placed in the coldest area of the fridge.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-004 — The front corners and sides become warm and form condensation

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-004`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations.  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 64.  

**Customer phrasing:** The front corners and sides become warm and form condensation; How to fix the front corners and sides become warm and form condensation.  

**Documented checks and actions:**
1. Understand that heat-proof piping may cause warmth in high ambient temperatures; this is not a system failure.
2. Ensure the door is closed properly to prevent condensation.
3. Recognize that condensation may occur when humid air contacts the cool surface.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-005 — The ice maker does not make ice

**Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-005`  
**Evidence:** [DOCUMENTED]  
**Applies to:** Samsung free-standing refrigerator configurations.  
**Source:** Refrigerator — User manual (Free Standing Appliance), PDF page(s) 64.  

**Customer phrasing:** The ice maker does not make ice; How to fix the ice maker does not make ice.  

**Documented checks and actions:**
1. Wait for 12 hours if the ice bucket is empty.
2. Check if the Ice Maker Off icon is lit; if so, press and hold it for 3 seconds.
3. Ensure the freezer temperature is set below 32 °F (0 °C).
4. Verify that the water line is connected and the shut-off valve is open.
5. Check for ice clumps in the ice bucket and empty if necessary.

## Troubleshooting: Moisture, Controls, Doors and Indicators

This section provides troubleshooting guidance for various issues related to the GE GNE27JYMFS bottom-freezer refrigerator. Each symptom is accompanied by possible causes and recommended actions.

### GE-SYMPTOM-023 — Interior Light Does Not Work

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-023`  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9.  
**Customer symptom:** Interior light does not work.  

**Documented cause/check pairs:**
- **Possible cause:** No power at outlet.  
  **Documented check/action:** Replace fuse or reset the breaker.
- **Possible cause:** LED lights are out.  
  **Documented check/action:** Call Service - see Replacing the Lights.

### GE-SYMPTOM-028 — Actual Temperature Not Equal to Set Temperature

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-028`  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9.  
**Customer symptom:** Actual temperature not equal to set temperature.  

**Documented cause/check pairs:**
- **Possible cause:** Unit just plugged in.  
  **Documented check/action:** Allow 24 hours for system to stabilize.
- **Possible cause:** Door open for too long.  
  **Documented check/action:** Allow 24 hours for system to stabilize.
- **Possible cause:** Warm food added to refrigerator.  
  **Documented check/action:** Allow 24 hours for system to stabilize.
- **Possible cause:** Defrost cycle is in process.  
  **Documented check/action:** Allow 24 hours for system to stabilize.

### GE-SYMPTOM-031 — Food Freezing in the Refrigerator

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-031`  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9.  
**Customer symptom:** Food freezing in the refrigerator.  

**Documented cause/check pairs:**
- **Possible cause:** Food too close to the air vent at the back of the refrigerator.  
  **Documented check/action:** Move the food away from the air vent.
- **Possible cause:** Refrigerator control is set too cold.  
  **Documented check/action:** Move the refrigerator control to a warmer temperature setting one increment at a time.

### GE-SYMPTOM-032 — Water is Leaking from Dispenser

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-032`  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9.  
**Customer symptom:** Water is leaking from dispenser.  

**Documented cause/check pairs:**
- **Possible cause:** Glass not being held under the dispenser long enough after button is released.  
  **Documented check/action:** Hold the glass underneath the dispenser for 2–3 seconds after releasing the dispenser button.
- **Possible cause:** Air may be present in the water line system, causing water to drip after being dispensed.  
  **Documented check/action:** Dispense water for at least 2 minutes to remove air from system.

### GE-SYMPTOM-035 — Water Filter Indicator Light Remains Red Even After Replacing the Water Filter

**Knowledge ID:** `GE-SYMPTOM-035`  
**Applies to:** GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators of 18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft in publication 49-1000472 Rev. 9.  
**Customer symptom:** Water filter indicator light remains red even after replacing the water filter.  

**Documented cause/check pairs:**
- **Possible cause:** Improperly installed filter.  
  **Documented check/action:** Verify you have the XWFE filter and not XWF. Replace filter if label shows damage.
- **Possible cause:** Water filter indicator must be reset (on some models).  
  **Documented check/action:** Press and hold the WATER FILTER pad for 3 seconds.

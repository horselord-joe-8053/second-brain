# Warranty, Regulatory Information, and Open Source Software for Samsung RF28K9070SG

This document provides detailed information regarding the warranty, regulatory compliance, and open source software associated with the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator.

## Warranty Information

### U.S. Warranty Terms
- **Coverage**: One year for parts and labor; five years for the sealed refrigeration system; ten years for the digital inverter compressor.
- **Source**: User manual, pages 68-70.
- **Conditions**: Warranty applies to products purchased and used in the U.S. under specified conditions.

### Canadian Warranty Terms
- **Coverage**: One year of in-home labor and parts; ten years for the inverter compressor.
- **Source**: User manual, pages 71-72.
- **Conditions**: Warranty applies to products purchased and used in Canada.

## Regulatory Compliance

- **Compliance**: The refrigerator complies with FCC and IC regulations regarding wireless communication.
- **Source**: User manual, pages 14-15.

## Open Source Software Information

- **Licensing**: Information about open-source software licenses included in the product.
- **Source**: User manual, pages 73-76.

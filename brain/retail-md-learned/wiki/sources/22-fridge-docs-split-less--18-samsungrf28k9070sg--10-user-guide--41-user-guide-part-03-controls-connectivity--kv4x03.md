# Controls, Operation, Features, Storage and Care

This section provides detailed information on the controls and features of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, including temperature management, connectivity options, and maintenance procedures.

## Feature Panel Overview

### Feature Panel Identification
- **Freezer / Power Freeze**
- **Fridge / Power Cool**
- **Door Alarm**
- **FlexZone**
- **Lighting / Control Lock**
- **Ice Maker Off**
- **Cubed Ice / Override**
- **Crushed Ice / Filter Reset**
- **Network Connection**

### Temperature Display Behavior
When settings change, the panel continues to show the measured compartment temperature until it reaches the set target, then displays the chosen value. This delay is normal; repeated adjustments are unnecessary.

## Temperature Control Features

### Freezer Setting and Power Freeze
- **Set Temperature:** Press **Freezer** repeatedly to select a temperature from **5°F (−15°C) to −8°F (−23°C)**.
- **Power Freeze Activation:** Hold **Freezer** for **3 seconds** to activate; hold again for 3 seconds to deactivate. It runs the freezer at full power temporarily.

### Fridge Temperature and Power Cool
- **Set Temperature:** Press **Fridge** repeatedly; selectable values are **34–44°F (1–7°C)**.
- **Power Cool Activation:** Hold **Fridge** for **3 seconds** to activate or deactivate. It temporarily runs cooling at full power.

## FlexZone Feature

### FlexZone Modes
The FlexZone compartment can be set to different temperatures for various food types:
- **Freezer:** Same as freezer compartment
- **Soft Freezing:** **23°F (−5°C)** for meat and fish
- **Chill / Meat:** **30°F (−1°C)** for deli items
- **Cool:** **41°F (5°C)** for beverages

### Mode Selection
Hold **FlexZone** for **3 seconds**, then press repeatedly to cycle through modes.

## SmartThings Connectivity

### SmartThings App Setup
To connect the refrigerator to the SmartThings app:
1. Install the SmartThings app on a supported mobile device.
2. Use a Samsung account to register the refrigerator.

### Troubleshooting Connectivity Issues
If connection fails, verify:
- Home wireless access point/router operation
- Account login
- Compatible app/device and refrigerator registration

## Smart Grid Demand Response

### Demand Response Functionality
This feature allows the refrigerator to adjust energy consumption based on utility signals. 

### Override Activation
Hold **Cubed Ice** for **3 seconds** to activate or deactivate the override mode.

## Maintenance Procedures

### Cleaning and Care
- Unplug the power cord before cleaning.
- Use a moistened soft lint-free cloth for interior/exterior cleaning.
- Avoid spraying water directly onto the refrigerator.

### Component Handling
Guidelines for removing and replacing shelves, drawers, and bins are provided to ensure proper maintenance.

### Wine Rack Installation
The wine rack can be positioned in various locations depending on the model. Always empty the rack before removal or insertion.

### Safe Cleaning Practices
Do not use strong detergents or spray water directly into dispenser openings to avoid damage.

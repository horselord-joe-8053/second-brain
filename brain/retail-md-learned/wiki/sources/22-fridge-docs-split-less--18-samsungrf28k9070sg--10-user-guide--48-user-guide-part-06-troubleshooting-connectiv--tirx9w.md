# Troubleshooting Connectivity for Samsung RF28K9070SG

This document provides troubleshooting steps for connectivity issues related to the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, focusing on the SmartThings app and Energy Management System (EMS).

## Troubleshooting Symptoms

### SAM-SYMPTOM-011 — Could not find “SmartThings” in the app market
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-011`
- **Customer phrasing:** Could not find “SmartThings” in the app market; How to fix could not find “smartthings” in the app market.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - The SmartThings app is designed for Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or later, iOS 10.0 or later, iPhone 6 or later, and is optimized for Samsung smartphones (Galaxy S and Galaxy Note series).

### SAM-SYMPTOM-012 — The SmartThings app fails to operate
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-012`
- **Customer phrasing:** The SmartThings app fails to operate; How to fix the smartthings app fails to operate.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - The SmartThings app is available for applicable models only.
  - The old Samsung Smart Refrigerator app cannot connect with Samsung Smart Home models.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-013 — The SmartThings app is installed but is not connected to my refrigerator
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-013`
- **Customer phrasing:** The SmartThings app is installed but is not connected to my refrigerator; How to fix the smartthings app is installed but is not connected to my refrigerator.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - You must log into your Samsung account to use the app.
  - Make sure that your router is operating normally.
  - If you have not connected your refrigerator to the SmartThings App after the app was installed, you must make the connection using the device registration function of the app.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-014 — Could not log into the app
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-014`
- **Customer phrasing:** Could not log into the app; How to fix could not log into the app.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - You must log into your Samsung account to use the app.
  - If you don’t have a Samsung account, follow the app’s onscreen instructions to create one.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-015 — An error message appears when I try to register my refrigerator
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-015`
- **Customer phrasing:** An error message appears when I try to register my refrigerator; How to fix an error message appears when I try to register my refrigerator.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - Easy Connection may fail due to the distance from your access point (AP) or electrical interference from the surrounding environment. Wait a moment and try again.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-016 — The SmartThings app is successfully connected to my refrigerator but does not run
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-016`
- **Customer phrasing:** The SmartThings app is successfully connected to my refrigerator but does not run; How to fix the smartthings app is successfully connected to my refrigerator but does not run.
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - Exit and restart the SmartThings app or disconnect and reconnect the router.
  - Unplug the power cord of the refrigerator, and then plug it in again after 1 minute.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-017 — What Do I Need to use the Energy Management and Smart Grid functions?
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-017`
- **Customer phrasing:** What Do I Need to use the Energy Management and Smart Grid functions?; How to fix what do I need to use the energy management and smart grid functions?
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - To use the Smart Grid (Demand Response) and Energy Management functions on your refrigerator, you need:
    - Wireless access point (router)
    - Samsung Energy Management-supported refrigerator
    - Smartphone Registration
    - Connect your refrigerator to your home Wi-Fi network.
    - Register for the EMS service with your electric company that has an EMS (Energy Management System) supporting SEP (Smart Energy profile).
    - Download the SmartThings app from the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or Samsung Galaxy Apps.
    - Install and run the SmartThings app on your smartphone.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-018 — Why isn’t the Energy Management function working normally?
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-018`
- **Customer phrasing:** Why isn’t the Energy Management function working normally?; How to fix why isn’t the energy management function working normally?
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - Make sure that your home router is operating normally with proper Internet service and connection.
  - Make sure that the refrigerator is connected to the AP. Checkpoints:
    - Connect your smartphone to the router (AP, Access Point), and then check if you can browse the Internet on the smartphone.
    - Check if the Wi-Fi icon on the refrigerator’s display is on. If not, connect the refrigerator to the AP.

### SAM-SYMPTOM-019 — Why isn’t the Delay Defrost Capability working normally?
- **Knowledge ID:** `SAM-SYMPTOM-019`
- **Customer phrasing:** Why isn’t the Delay Defrost Capability working normally?; How to fix why isn’t the delay defrost capability working normally?
- **Documented checks and actions:**
  - Make sure that your home router is operating normally with proper Internet service and connection.
  - Make sure that the refrigerator does not display L3 or L4 on the display panel.

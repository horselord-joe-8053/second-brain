## Overview

This document serves as a troubleshooting guide for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, detailing common customer symptoms and the corresponding checks to diagnose and resolve issues. It includes specific troubleshooting procedures for various symptoms, emphasizing the importance of following manufacturer guidelines.

## Troubleshooting Procedures

### 1. Warm Front Corners and Condensation
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-02
- **Symptoms:** The front corners and sides become warm and form condensation.
- **Checks:**
  - Ensure the door is closed properly.
  - Verify that the ambient temperature is not excessively high.
  - Confirm that the heat-proof piping is functioning.

### 2. Ice Maker Malfunction
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-03
- **Symptoms:** The ice maker does not make ice.
- **Checks:**
  - Wait 12 hours if the ice bucket is empty.
  - Ensure the Ice Maker Off icon is not lit.
  - Check the freezer temperature and water tank levels.

### 3. Non-Functioning Water Dispenser
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-04
- **Symptoms:** The water dispenser is not functioning.
- **Checks:**
  - Inspect the water tank for freezing.
  - Ensure the water tank is filled and installed correctly.

### 4. Bubbling Sound
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-06
- **Symptoms:** You hear a bubbling sound.
- **Checks:**
  - This is normal; it indicates refrigerant circulation.

### 5. Bad Smell Inside Refrigerator
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-07
- **Symptoms:** A bad smell comes from inside the refrigerator.
- **Checks:**
  - Check for spoiled food and clean the refrigerator regularly.

### 6. Frost on Interior Walls
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-08
- **Symptoms:** Frost forms on the interior walls.
- **Checks:**
  - Ensure no food blocks the vents and that the door is closed properly.

### 7. Condensation on Interior Walls
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-07-09
- **Symptoms:** Condensation forms on the interior walls or around vegetables.
- **Checks:**
  - Cover food items to prevent moisture accumulation.

### 8. Samsung Smart Home App Issues
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-03
- **Symptoms:** Could not find “Samsung Smart Home” in the app market.
- **Checks:**
  - Ensure your smartphone meets the app requirements.

### 9. App Fails to Operate
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-04
- **Symptoms:** The Samsung Smart Home app fails to operate.
- **Checks:**
  - Confirm compatibility with your refrigerator model.

### 10. App Not Connected
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-05
- **Symptoms:** The Samsung Smart Home app is installed but is not connected to my refrigerator.
- **Checks:**
  - Log into your Samsung account and check router functionality.

### 11. Login Issues
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-06
- **Symptoms:** Could not log into the app.
- **Checks:**
  - Follow the app’s instructions to create a Samsung account if needed.

### 12. Registration Error
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-07
- **Symptoms:** An error message appears when I try to register my refrigerator.
- **Checks:**
  - Ensure proximity to the access point and check for interference.

### 13. App Not Running
- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-SYMPTOM-08-08
- **Symptoms:** The Samsung Smart Home app is successfully connected to my refrigerator but does not run.
- **Checks:**
  - Restart the app and check the refrigerator's power connection.

# Controls, Operation, Features, Storage and Care

This section of the user guide for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator provides detailed information on the controls, operation, and maintenance of the appliance. It covers essential features, temperature settings, and care instructions to ensure optimal performance.

## Temperature Controls

The refrigerator is equipped with electronically operated temperature controls that display actual and set temperatures. The manual provides recommended settings and procedures for adjusting temperatures. 

- **Cooling ON/OFF:** A powered display does not guarantee that refrigeration is enabled. If cooling is off, the refrigerator and freezer will not cool. 
- **Recommended Temperature Settings:**
  - Refrigerator: 33°F to 45°F (0°C to 7°C)
  - Freezer: -5°F to 5°F (-21°C to -15°C)

## Max Ice Feature

The Max Ice feature temporarily increases ice production, which is beneficial for users needing extra ice quickly. 

- **Activation Procedure:** Press the OPTIONS button to enter Options mode, then press the TEMPERATURE button to activate Max Ice.

## Sabbath Mode

Sabbath Mode modifies the refrigerator's operation to comply with Sabbath observance. 

- **Important Limitation:** A power outage during Sabbath Mode may not be indicated, so the absence of a power-outage icon cannot confirm uninterrupted power.

## Crispers and Humidity Control

Crisper drawers are designed to store fruits and vegetables with regulated humidity to enhance freshness. 

- **Humidity Regulation:** The humidity level in the crisper drawers is automatically regulated, and there is no user humidity slider.

## Condenser Maintenance

Routine cleaning of the condenser is not necessary under normal conditions. However, in particularly dusty or greasy environments, cleaning every 2-3 months is recommended.

- **Cleaning Procedure:**
  1. Unplug the refrigerator.
  2. Remove the base grille.
  3. Vacuum accessible areas with a soft brush.
  4. Replace the grille and reconnect.

## Additional Features

The guide also includes information on door operation, adjustable shelves, food storage guidelines, and cleaning recommendations.

- **Door Operation:** The refrigerator doors can be opened separately or together, and proper closure is essential for maintaining cooling efficiency.
- **Adjustable Shelves:** Instructions for removing and adjusting shelves are provided, emphasizing safety precautions.

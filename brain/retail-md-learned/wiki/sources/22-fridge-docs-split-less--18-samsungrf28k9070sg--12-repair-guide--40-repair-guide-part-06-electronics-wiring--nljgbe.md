## Overview

This repair guide focuses on the electronics wiring of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator model. It provides essential information regarding the main board, SMPS, inverter board, TDM four-way valve, and wiring diagrams necessary for effective troubleshooting and repair.

## Main Board, SMPS, and Inverter Board

### Identification and Safety

- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-ELECTRONICS-001
- **Applies to:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381, RF28K9380
- **Source:** Fast Track Troubleshooting, PDF page(s) 11

The guide includes an annotated layout of the main board, SMPS, and inverter board, emphasizing the importance of high voltage caution during repairs. It warns that AC input power and high voltage are generated at the SMPS PCB and inverter-PCB power terminals.

**Visual Reference:** For exact physical board connector positions and printed terminal labels, refer to the **Main Board of Fridge Layout** and photographs under “SMPS” and “Inverter Board (Compressor)” on printed page 11 of *Fast Track Troubleshooting*.

## TDM Four-Way Valve

### Function and Operation

- **Knowledge ID:** SFT-TDM-VALVE-001
- **Applies to:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381, RF28K9380
- **Source:** Fast Track Troubleshooting, PDF page(s) 10

The TDM four-way valve is crucial for directing refrigerant flow within the refrigerator's cooling system. The guide describes its operation, including the natural defrosting process, which raises humidity by running the fan when cooling is inactive.

**Visual Reference:** See the **unlabeled valve schematic** and photographs under “TDM 4 Way Valve” on printed page 10 of *Fast Track Troubleshooting*.

### Electrical Routing

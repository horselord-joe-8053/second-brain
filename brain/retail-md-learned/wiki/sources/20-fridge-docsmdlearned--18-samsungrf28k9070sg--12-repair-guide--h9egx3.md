# Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide

The Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide is a comprehensive document designed for trained technicians, detailing troubleshooting procedures, electrical safety measures, diagnostic modes, and error codes specific to the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator model. This guide is derived from the *Fast Track Troubleshooting* service-data sheet and emphasizes the importance of safety and proper handling of electrical components.

## Electrical Safety

- Guidelines and precautions for safely handling electrical components in appliances.
- Emphasizes the need for trained technicians to prevent injury or damage during repairs.

## Diagnostic Modes

- Specific operational modes that technicians can access to diagnose issues in the refrigerator.
- Provides a structured approach to troubleshooting and identifying faults.

### Mode Entry Steps

| Mode | Button Sequence | Indication / Exit |
|---|---|---|
| Demo / Cooling Off | Hold **Freezer + Fridge + Door Alarm 6 seconds** | `0–FF` displayed; repeat combination to exit |
| Load Mode | Hold **Freezer + Fridge + Flex Zone 6 seconds** | All ON/OFF LEDs blink; release to display operating-load status |
| Diagnostic / Error display | Hold **Freezer + Fridge 6 seconds** until display blinks | Automatically exits after **1 minute** |
| Force / Defrost | Hold **Freezer + Flex Zone 6 seconds** until display blank and beep | Modes **1–5** shown as segment pictures |

## Error Codes

- Codes generated by the refrigerator to indicate specific faults or issues.
- Critical for diagnosing problems and guiding repair actions.

### Diagnostic Mode Codes: Sensor Faults

| Error Code | Manual Item | PCB Contact / Reference | Printed Expected Range |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freezer sensor | CN30 pins **1–3** | **4.5–1.0 V DC** |
| 2 | Fridge sensor | CN30 **1–5** | **4.5–1.0 V DC** |
| 4 | Freezer defrost sensor | CN30 **1–4** | **4.5–1.0 V DC** |
| 5 | Fridge defrost sensor | CN30 **1–6** | **4.5–1.0 V DC** |

## General Symptoms

- Common issues and first checks for troubleshooting.

| User Symptom | Source-Listed First Check |
|---|---|
| No new ice | If bin emptied, allow **12 hours** to replenish. |
| Water dispenser not working | Check frozen/missing/incorrectly installed water tank and filter. |

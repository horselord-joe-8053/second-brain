# Water and Ice Filter User Guide

This user guide provides essential operational procedures and maintenance instructions for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator's water and ice dispenser, including filter replacement and troubleshooting tips.

## Water and Ice Dispenser Operation

The water and ice dispenser operates by selecting the appropriate lever for dispensing. To avoid spills and manage taste issues after periods of inactivity, follow these guidelines:

- Select chilled water, cubed ice, or crushed ice as appropriate.
- For combined ice and water, place the glass under the **ice lever (A)** and press, then under **water lever (B)** and press. If both are pressed at once, only the first lever operates.
- Wait **1 second** after dispensing before removing the glass.
- If water has not been dispensed for **2–3 days**, the first **1–2 glasses** may have an abnormal taste or odor; discard those initial glasses.

## Water Filter Replacement

Proper water filter replacement is crucial for maintaining water quality. Follow these steps for replacing the water filter and resetting the filter indicator:

1. Unlock the old cartridge by turning its knob **90° counterclockwise**.
2. Pull it straight out, keeping it aligned to avoid leaks.
3. Insert the replacement and turn clockwise to lock.
4. Hold **Crushed Ice for 3 seconds** to reset the filter-life detector.
5. Open the water valve and run water through the dispenser for approximately **7 minutes** to purge air and impurities.

**Note:** Use only Samsung-provided or approved replacement filters.

## Ice Bucket Management

To prevent clumping and ensure proper ice production, follow these instructions for removing and reseating the ice bucket:

1. Hold the bottom and side of the bucket; gently lift and pull it out.
2. Remove remaining cubes and reinstall.
3. If the bucket cannot seat firmly, turn the **ice screw 90 degrees** and try again.

## Reverse Osmosis Water Pressure Requirements

When using a reverse osmosis system with the refrigerator, ensure the following water pressure conditions:

- The supply to the reverse-osmosis system must be **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)**.
- The filtered line feeding the refrigerator must be **at least 40 psi (276 kPa)**.

## Temporary Cloudiness in Dispensed Water

Dispensed water may appear cloudy due to the filtering process, which can temporarily saturate water with oxygen and nitrogen. This cloudiness should clear **after a few seconds** and is not indicative of a failure.

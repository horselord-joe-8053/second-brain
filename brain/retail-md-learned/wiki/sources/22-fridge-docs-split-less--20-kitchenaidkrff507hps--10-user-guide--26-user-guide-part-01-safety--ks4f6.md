# KitchenAid KRFF507HPS User Guide: Part 01 - Safety

This document provides essential safety guidelines for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator, focusing on electrical safety, child entrapment prevention, and glass safety.

## Electrical Safety Precautions

- **Required Electrical Supply:** 
  - 115 V, 60 Hz, AC-only, 15 or 20 A fused, grounded supply.
  - Use a grounded three-prong outlet; avoid extension cords, adapters, or removed ground prongs.

- **Warnings:**
  - Do not remove the ground prong.
  - Ensure the refrigerator is disconnected from power before cleaning or maintenance.

## Child Entrapment Safety

- **Preventing Entrapment:**
  - Follow instructions for safely discarding old refrigerators to prevent children from becoming trapped.
  - Always keep doors closed when not in use.

## Glass Safety

- **Handling Glass Components:**
  - Do not clean cold glass shelves with warm water to avoid breakage.
  - Tempered glass can shatter; support shelves with both hands when handling.

## Customer Support Boundaries

- Do not propose internal wiring changes or bypass grounding as troubleshooting options.

## Additional Documented Conditions

- All safety messages will follow the safety alert symbol and either the word “DANGER” or “WARNING.”
- Specific warnings regarding electrical connections, moving the refrigerator, and handling glass components are detailed throughout the guide.

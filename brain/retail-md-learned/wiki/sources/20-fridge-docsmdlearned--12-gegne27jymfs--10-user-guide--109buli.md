# GE / GE Profile Bottom-Freezer Refrigerators: Owner's Manual & Installation Instructions

This manual provides essential information for the operation, installation, and maintenance of GE and GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators, specifically model GNE27JYMFS. It covers various features, safety protocols, and troubleshooting guidelines.

## Coverage and Model Applicability

The manual addresses multiple configurations, including single-door and French-door refrigerators, and outlines the importance of verifying the exact model identifier and features before providing instructions.

## Safety Protocols

- **Electrical Safety**: Proper installation and grounding are crucial. Use a grounded outlet and avoid using adapters or extension cords.
- **Before Cleaning/Repair**: Unplug the unit and ensure all parts are replaced before operation. Repairs should be conducted by qualified personnel.
- **Water Supply**: Ensure a potable cold water supply within the specified pressure range (25–120 psi).

## Temperature Control

- **Rotary Models**: Default settings for refrigerator and freezer temperatures are provided, along with stabilization times after adjustments.
- **Top-Control Models**: Instructions for adjusting temperatures and using features like TurboCool and Door Alarm are included.

## Sabbath Mode

Details on how to activate and deactivate Sabbath Mode, which disables certain functions for religious observance, are provided.

## Water Dispenser and Icemaker Operation

Guidelines for operating the water dispenser and icemaker, including installation and maintenance of the XWFE water filter, are outlined.

## Customer Support and Troubleshooting

A troubleshooting table addresses common customer complaints and the appropriate first actions to take, along with warranty information.

## Important Distinctions

- **Model Specificity**: Not all features are present in every model variant; instructions must be tailored to the specific model.
- **Warranty Information**: Coverage details and limitations are provided, emphasizing the importance of proof of purchase for claims.

## Contact Information

For customer support, the manual provides contact details for service and parts in the US and Canada.

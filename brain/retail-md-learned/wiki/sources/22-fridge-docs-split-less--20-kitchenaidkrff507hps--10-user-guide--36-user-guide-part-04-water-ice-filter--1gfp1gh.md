# Water and Ice Filter User Guide

This section of the user guide for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator focuses on the installation, replacement, and maintenance of water and ice filters, as well as the operation of the water and ice dispensers.

## Water Filter Installation and Replacement

The guide provides detailed instructions for installing and replacing the refrigerator's water filter to ensure a clean water supply. 

1. **Locate the water filter housing**.
2. **Release the old cartridge** by turning it a quarter turn.
3. **Fit the new cartridge** into the housing and turn to lock.
4. **Flush the system** according to the printed water-dispensing procedure.
5. **Reset the filter-status indicator** as per the installed controls.

**Important Notes:**
- Replace the water filter approximately every six months.
- Ensure the filter is correctly seated to avoid water flow issues.

## Water Filtration Performance

The water filter is certified for its capacity and effectiveness against various contaminants. 

- **Rated Capacity**: 200 gallons (757 liters).
- **Performance Data**:
  - Chlorine taste/odor: 97.0% average reduction.
  - Lead reduction: greater than 99.3% at pH 6.5.
  - Cyst reduction: greater than 99.99%.

Refer to the performance data sheet for detailed test results and conditions.

## Air Filter Installation and Replacement

Instructions for replacing the air filter are provided to help control odors within the refrigerator.

1. **Identify the installed housing** for the air filter.
2. **Remove the used air filter** by squeezing the side tabs.
3. **Install the new air filter** according to the specified orientation.

**Replacement Interval**: Every six months.

## Water/Ice Dispenser Calibration

The guide explains how to calibrate the measured fill feature of the water and ice dispensers.

1. **Check the filter and water supply** before calibration.
2. **Use a measuring cup** to dispense water accurately.
3. **Follow the control panel sequence** for calibration.

**Troubleshooting Tips**:
- Ensure the door is closed and the control lock is disengaged.
- Flush the water system after connecting to a new water source.

## Ice Maker Operation

The guide outlines the operation and maintenance of the ice maker.

1. **Confirm the ice maker is ON** and that the water supply is connected.
2. **Check the ice storage bin** for proper seating.
3. **Follow the printed instructions** for cleaning and maintenance.

**Important Notes**:
- Allow 24 hours for the first batch of ice to be produced.
- Discard the first three batches of ice produced.

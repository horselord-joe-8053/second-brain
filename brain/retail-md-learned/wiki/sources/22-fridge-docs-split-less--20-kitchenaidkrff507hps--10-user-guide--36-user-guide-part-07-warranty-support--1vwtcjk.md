## Warranty Coverage
The KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator comes with a one-year limited warranty for parts and labor, along with a ten-year limited warranty for the sealed system. This warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use.

### Exclusions from Warranty
Certain conditions are not covered under the warranty, including:
- Improper maintenance
- Commercial use
- External plumbing or electrical corrections

### Warranty Administration
To obtain warranty service, customers must provide:
- Receipt or proof of purchase
- Model and serial number
- Detailed description of the problem

Authorized KitchenAid Service Providers conduct covered warranty work.

## Customer Support
Customers can contact KitchenAid support for assistance with inquiries and issues. The following contact information is provided:
- **United States:** 1-800-422-1230
- **Canada:** 1-800-807-6777

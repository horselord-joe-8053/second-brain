# GE Refrigerator Safety and Service Guidelines

This document outlines essential safety and operational guidelines for GE / GE Profile bottom-freezer refrigerators. It emphasizes the importance of following safety precautions to prevent injuries and hazards associated with the use of these appliances.

## Safety Precautions

- Users must avoid contact with cold surfaces and ensure proper grounding to prevent electrical shock.
- Keep fingers clear of door pinch points and avoid touching freezer surfaces with wet hands.
- Do not expose cold glass shelves to warm water or impact, as they may break.
- Follow specific guidelines for the disposal of old refrigerators to prevent child entrapment.

## Electrical Grounding

- The refrigerator must be connected to a grounded three-prong outlet.
- Removal of the ground prong, use of adapter plugs, or extension cords is prohibited.
- If only a two-prong outlet is available, a qualified electrician should install a properly grounded three-prong outlet.

## Child Safety and Entrapment Warnings

- Guidelines are provided to prevent children from being trapped in appliances.
- It is crucial to keep children away from abandoned appliances and to follow documented disposal precautions.

## Flammable Refrigerants

- Some GE refrigerators use R600a (Isobutane), which is a flammable refrigerant.
- Users are warned of fire or explosion hazards associated with R600a and are advised to avoid damaging refrigeration tubing.
- Only authorized service personnel should handle repairs involving R600a.

## Service Limitations

- The document does not provide complete technical details for servicing internal components.
- Users are encouraged to seek professional assistance for repairs and servicing.

## Knowledge IDs

- **GE-SAFETY-001**: Safety, electrical grounding, and child-entrapment warnings.
- **GE-R600A-001**: Flammable R600a refrigerant safety and authorized service.

## Additional Information

- A change on the temperature control panel does not disconnect power to the unit; physically disconnect electricity before service.
- Follow federal and local regulations for the disposal of refrigerators containing R600a.

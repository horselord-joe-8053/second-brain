# Fast Track Troubleshooting

This document serves as a guide for troubleshooting specific Samsung refrigerator models: RF28K9070, RF22K9381, and RF28K9380. It outlines systematic procedures to diagnose and resolve issues effectively.

## Document Identity, Scope, and Answerability Policy

- **Document Title:** Fast Track Troubleshooting
- **Document Scope:** Models RF28K9070, RF22K9381, and RF28K9380 as printed on this service sheet; individual procedures may be feature-specific.
- **Reference:** Samsung Fast Track Troubleshooting, production DRRF28K9070-060616, 12-page PDF.

## Response Rule for Knowledge Base

1. Identify the refrigerator model and fitted feature first.
2. Prefer directly applicable `[DOCUMENTED]` instructions for the answerable part of a question.
3. Use `[UNKNOWN]` only for unsupported information.
4. A missing answer in a retrieved shard alone is not evidence that the appliance lacks a feature.
5. Avoid turning "some models" or ambiguous applicability into a firm compatibility claim.

## Evidence Labels

- **[DOCUMENTED]:** Information directly supported by this manual, including faithful explanations of printed or visually verified instructions.
- **[UNKNOWN]:** Information not established in the reviewed source or remains uncertain.

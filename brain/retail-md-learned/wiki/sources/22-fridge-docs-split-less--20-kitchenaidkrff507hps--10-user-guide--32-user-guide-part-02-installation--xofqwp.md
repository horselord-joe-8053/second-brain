# Installation, Water Pressure, Doors and Leveling

This section provides detailed installation procedures for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator, including unpacking, water pressure requirements, door removal and reattachment, and leveling adjustments.

## Unpacking, Placement and Moving the Refrigerator

### Unpacking
- Remove tape/glue using a small amount of liquid dish soap and warm water, then dry.
- Avoid abrasive products, alcohol, and flammable cleaners.
- Remove/recycle packaging and clean before use.

### Moving
- Cover floors with hardboard or cardboard.
- Pull the appliance straight out and do not “walk” or wiggle it to avoid damaging the flooring.
- Measure the intended doorway to determine if door removal is necessary.

### Door Removal Prerequisites
- Turn controls OFF, unplug, remove food and loose door bins, and use supported lifting arrangements.

## Water Pressure Requirements

A cold-water connection at **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)** is required for the dispenser and ice maker. Lower pressure can reduce water flow and create hollow or irregular ice cubes.

### Reverse Osmosis Considerations
- Check a blocked sediment filter and allow the RO storage tank to refill after heavy use.
- For water-pressure uncertainty, contact a licensed qualified plumber.

## Connecting the Water Line and Initial Fill

### Before Connecting
- Unplug/disconnect power; shut off main water; open the nearest faucet to clear the line.
- Follow the connection instructions specific to your model.

### Critical Precautions
- If switched on before the water connection is installed, leave the ice maker OFF.
- Tighten connections but do not overtighten.

## Door Removal and Reattachment

### Use Case
- If the cabinet cannot pass through a doorway, the manual shows right- and left-hand door removal/reinstallation as separate sequences.

### Required Tools
- **3/16-inch hex-key wrench**
- **#2 Phillips screwdriver**

### Instructions
- Disconnect power and keep doors supported.
- Do not rely on magnetic gaskets to hold a door while hinges are loose.

## Leveling, Closing and Aligning Doors

### Adjustments
- Adjust cabinet tilt/leveling and check door alignment.
- Use a **1/4-inch hex driver** to turn both brake feet clockwise to raise the front and make doors close more readily.

### Door Alignment
- The right refrigerator door has an adjustment inside the bottom hinge pin, operated with the short end of the **1/8-inch hex key**.

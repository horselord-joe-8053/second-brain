## Water Dispenser Operation

The document outlines the operation of the water dispenser, emphasizing the need to purge air from the system after installation and the importance of regular cleaning of the spill tray. 

- **Initial Purge**: If no water appears after first installation, trapped air may need purging. Press the dispenser paddle for at least five minutes to remove trapped air and fill the system, then discard the first six full glasses to flush impurities.
- **Cleaning**: The spill shelf is not self-draining; it needs regular cleaning.

## Ice Maker Operation

The document states that a newly installed refrigerator may take 12-24 hours to start producing ice, with an estimated output of 10 cubes per cycle. 

- **Initial Operation**: Verify that the icemaker is enabled, the water connection is secure, and the freezer temperature is appropriate. 

## XWFE Water Filter

The document details the location, replacement procedure, and performance specifications of the XWFE water filter, including its certification and capacity.

- **Location**: The XWFE cartridge holder is in the upper-right rear of the refrigerator compartment on fitted variants.
- **Replacement Procedure**: Locate and remove the cartridge using the illustrated locking method, fit the correct replacement/bypass plug, reseat and flush according to the installation directions, then reset the replacement indicator using the panel appropriate to the model.

## Performance Specifications

The XWFE filter is certified against multiple NSF standards, with specific performance metrics provided, such as flow rate and capacity.

- **Certification**: The filter is certified by IAPMO R&T against NSF/ANSI 42, 53, 401, and P473.
- **Capacity**: The filter has a capacity of 170 gallons (643.5 liters) and a service flow rate of 0.5 gpm (1.89 L/min).

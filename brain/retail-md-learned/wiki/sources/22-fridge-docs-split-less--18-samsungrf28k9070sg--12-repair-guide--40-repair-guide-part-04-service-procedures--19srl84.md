# Repair Guide: Part 04 - Service Procedures

This document provides detailed service procedures for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator model, including forced operation modes, ice maker operation, and removal procedures.

## Forced Operation Modes

### Overview
Forced operation modes allow technicians to manipulate the refrigerator's settings for troubleshooting purposes. 

### Procedure
1. Hold **Freezer + Flex Zone** for **6 seconds** until the screen goes blank and a beep is heard.
2. Press Flex Zone again to enter mode 1; successive mode selections are shown on the printed panel drawing.
3. After mode 5, the screen becomes blank; leaving it alone for **1 minute** returns to normal operation.

### Important Notes
- **FD** indicates defrost with **all heaters on**.
- The force-operation table provides 24-hour operating time for various configurations.

## Ice Maker Operation

### Overview
The ice maker operates based on specific logic that includes temperature checks and fault modes.

### Harvest Logic
- The board checks the eject thermistor after water filling. If the reading is below **18.5 degrees** for more than **5 seconds**, ice production is treated as completed.
- Harvest runs when the bucket is not sensed full.

### Technician ICE TEST
1. Hold **ICE TEST S/W** for at least **1.5 seconds**.
2. Below **0 degrees**, the heater turns on for about **2 minutes**; above **0 degrees**, for **30 seconds**.
3. After the heater stage, the harvest motor runs for about **3 minutes**.

## Ice Maker Removal Procedures

### Overview
This section outlines the step-by-step instructions for safely removing the ice maker and ice bucket.

### Removal Sequence
1. Wait **5 minutes** after manual defrost.
2. Lift and pull out the ice bucket.
3. Remove the wire-housing-cover screw and cover.
4. Disconnect the ice-maker housing connector.
5. Remove the duct-tray-ice screw.
6. Push the duct right to release the locking tab.
7. Withdraw the ice maker while pressing the hook.

### Safety Warning
Avoid damaging the tray grommets or refrigerant tube during removal.

## Auger Fan/Motor Assembly Removal

### Overview
Instructions for removing the auger assembly from the ice maker.

### Removal Procedure
1. Disconnect the **FAN-AUGER-ASSY connector**.
2. Hold the hook on the bottom of the **FAN-AUGER ASSY** and lift to free it from the locking tab.
3. Continue lifting to remove the assembly from the ice-maker compartment.

### Safety Note
Follow general repair safety restrictions during this procedure.

## Installation Guidelines

This section provides detailed instructions for properly installing GE bottom-freezer refrigerators, including site selection, dimensions, and clearance requirements. Proper installation is crucial to avoid operational issues and damage.

### Site Selection and Refrigerator Dimensions

- Ensure the refrigerator fits through doorways and into the designated space.
- Follow specific dimensional guidelines for various models (18, 19, 21, 22, 24, 25, 26, and 27 cu ft).
- Allow clearances for opening doors and water-line access.
- Confirm the floor can support the refrigerator's weight.

### Door Removal and Reinstallation

- Procedures for removing and reinstalling refrigerator doors, particularly for models with French doors.
- Necessary for narrow-entry installations and maintenance.
- Follow the numbered service steps for proper hinge support and wiring connections.

### Water Line Installation

- Instructions for connecting the refrigerator to a household water supply.
- Includes tubing and valve requirements.
- Essential for models with water dispensers or ice makers to function properly.

### Knowledge IDs

- **GE-INSTALL-LOCATION-001**: Site selection and refrigerator dimension families.
- **GE-DOOR-REMOVAL-001**: French-door removal and reinstall procedures.
- **GE-WATER-LINE-001**: Water installation, tubing approvals, and shutoff valve requirements.

### Additional Information

- Ensure the refrigerator is installed in a location where temperatures do not fall below 60°F (16°C) or exceed 100°F (37°C).
- Avoid installation in areas exposed to water or direct sunlight.

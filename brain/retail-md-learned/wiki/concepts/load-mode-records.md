# Load Mode Records

Load mode records refer to data related to the operational load of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, which can affect its performance. These records are crucial for diagnosing issues related to energy consumption and efficiency.

## Importance

- Helps in understanding the refrigerator's operational parameters.
- Aids in identifying potential performance issues.

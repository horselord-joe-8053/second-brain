# Leveling and Door Alignment  

Leveling and door alignment refer to the adjustments made to ensure that the refrigerator doors close properly and are aligned. Proper alignment affects both functionality and aesthetics.  

## Importance  
Maintaining proper leveling and alignment is essential for the refrigerator's performance and to prevent issues such as doors not closing correctly.  

## Key Aspects  
- Adjusting the refrigerator's feet to ensure it is level on a flat surface.  
- Aligning the doors using snap rings and gap adjustments to maintain an even appearance and functionality.  

Proper leveling prevents operational issues and ensures door functionality.

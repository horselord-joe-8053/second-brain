# Max Ice Feature

The Max Ice feature in the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator is designed to temporarily increase ice production. This feature is particularly useful for users who require a larger quantity of ice in a short period.

- **Activation:** To activate the Max Ice feature, users must press the OPTIONS button to enter Options mode, followed by pressing the TEMPERATURE button.

This feature enhances the refrigerator's functionality, catering to the needs of users during gatherings or events.

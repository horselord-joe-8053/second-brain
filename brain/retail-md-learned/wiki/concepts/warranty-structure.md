The warranty structure for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator outlines the terms and conditions under which the product is covered for defects. It includes a one-year limited warranty for parts and labor, as well as a ten-year limited warranty for the sealed system.

### Importance
Understanding the warranty structure is essential for consumers as it provides critical information regarding what is covered, the duration of coverage, and the exclusions that apply.

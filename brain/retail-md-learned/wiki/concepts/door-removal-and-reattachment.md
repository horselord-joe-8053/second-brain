# Door Removal and Reattachment  

Door removal and reattachment involves the procedures necessary for safely removing and reinstalling the refrigerator doors. This is particularly important when moving the appliance through doorways or for maintenance purposes.  

## Importance  
Following the correct procedures for door removal and reattachment is crucial to avoid damage to the refrigerator and ensure proper functionality.  

## Procedures  
Door removal and reattachment are essential procedures for the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, particularly when navigating narrow spaces. This process involves:  

- Detaching the refrigerator and freezer doors to facilitate movement.  
- Following illustrated instructions to ensure proper handling and reattachment.  
- Preserving electrical connectors and supporting door weight during the process.

# Water/Ice Dispenser Calibration

Water/Ice dispenser calibration is the process of adjusting the dispenser settings to ensure accurate dispensing of water and ice from the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator.

## Calibration Steps

1. Check the water filter and household water supply.
2. Use a measuring cup to dispense water accurately.
3. Follow the control panel sequence for calibration.

**Troubleshooting Tips**: Ensure the door is closed and the control lock is disengaged before starting the calibration process.

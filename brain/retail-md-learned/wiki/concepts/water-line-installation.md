# Water Line Installation  
Water line installation refers to the process of connecting a refrigerator to a household water supply for its ice and water dispensing features. This process includes the necessary tubing and valve requirements, which are essential for the operation of models equipped with water dispensers or ice makers. Proper installation is crucial for functionality and to prevent leaks.

## Key Requirements  
- **Water Pressure:** The refrigerator requires a water pressure of 20–125 psi for optimal operation.  
- **Connection Instructions:** Use a new hose set with specified fittings, ensuring that connections are tight but not overtightened.  
- **Flushing Procedure:** After installation, flush 1 gallon of water through the filter before use.

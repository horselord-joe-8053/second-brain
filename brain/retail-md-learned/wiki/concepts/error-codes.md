# Error Codes

Error codes are specific numerical indicators generated by appliances, such as the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator, to signal faults or issues. These codes are critical for diagnosing problems and guiding repair actions, as outlined in the *Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide*. The guide provides detailed information on interpreting these codes and the associated troubleshooting steps.

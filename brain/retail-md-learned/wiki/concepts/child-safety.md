# Child Safety and Entrapment Warnings

Child safety and entrapment warnings are guidelines designed to prevent children from being trapped in appliances. These warnings are crucial for protecting against potential accidents and injuries involving children. GE emphasizes the importance of following documented disposal precautions to keep children safe from abandoned appliances.

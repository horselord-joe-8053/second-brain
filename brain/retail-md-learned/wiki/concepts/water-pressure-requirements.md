# Water Pressure Requirements

Water pressure requirements refer to the necessary range of water pressure for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator's ice maker and dispenser. The required pressure is between **35–120 psi (241–827 kPa)**.

## Significance
Understanding the water pressure requirements is essential for ensuring the proper functionality of the refrigerator's ice maker and dispenser, preventing issues such as hollow ice cubes.

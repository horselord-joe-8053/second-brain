# Installation Procedures

Installation procedures encompass the steps required to properly set up the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator. This includes unpacking, connecting water lines, and leveling the appliance to ensure it operates correctly and safely.

## Importance
Following the correct installation procedures is vital to prevent damage and ensure the refrigerator functions efficiently.

The defrost cycle is a period during which a refrigerator temporarily stops cooling to melt frost buildup. Understanding the defrost cycle is crucial for diagnosing issues related to refrigerator operation, particularly when the appliance appears to be non-operational. This cycle is a common feature in modern refrigerators, including the GE / GE Profile models.

# Flammable Refrigerants

Flammable refrigerants are substances that can ignite under certain conditions, posing fire hazards during service and disposal. R600a (Isobutane) is an example of a flammable refrigerant used in some GE refrigerators. Safety protocols must be followed to prevent fire hazards, and only authorized personnel should handle repairs involving flammable refrigerants.

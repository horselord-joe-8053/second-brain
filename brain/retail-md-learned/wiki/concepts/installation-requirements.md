# Installation Requirements

Installation requirements refer to the guidelines necessary for the proper placement and setup of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. These guidelines ensure optimal performance and longevity of the appliance. Key requirements include:

- Placement on a solid, level surface.
- Avoidance of direct sunlight and heat sources.
- Adequate space for door opening and service.
- Temperature range for installation: **50–110°F (10–43°C)**.

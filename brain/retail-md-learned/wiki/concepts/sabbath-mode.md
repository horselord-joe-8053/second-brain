# Sabbath Mode  
Sabbath Mode is a specific operational feature in GE refrigerators and is also available in the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator. This mode alters the appliance's functionality to comply with Sabbath observance, catering to specific user needs and allowing users to maintain their religious practices without compromising the functionality of their refrigerator.

## Features  
- **Temperature Control:** Modifies how the refrigerator maintains temperature during Sabbath observance.  
- **Alarm Functions:** Adjusts alarm settings to prevent disturbances during the Sabbath.  
- **Lighting:** Alters lighting functions to comply with religious practices.  

## Important Limitations  
- Users should be aware that if a power outage occurs while the refrigerator is in Sabbath Mode, the absence of a power-outage icon may not indicate uninterrupted power.

# Water Dispenser

The Water Dispenser is a feature in certain GE refrigerator models that allows users to dispense cold water directly from the refrigerator. The user guide provides guidelines for operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting to ensure the dispenser functions correctly and efficiently.

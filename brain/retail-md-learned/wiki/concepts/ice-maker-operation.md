# Ice Maker Operation

Ice Maker Operation encompasses the functionality, mechanics, and maintenance of the refrigerator's ice-making feature. Understanding this operation is important for users to know how and when ice is produced, including the time it takes for a newly installed refrigerator to start producing ice and the estimated output per cycle.

## Key Points

1. Confirm the ice maker is ON and that the water supply is connected.
2. Check the ice storage bin for proper seating.
3. Follow the printed instructions for cleaning and maintenance.

**Important Note**: Allow 24 hours for the first batch of ice to be produced and discard the first three batches for optimal quality.

## Operational Logic

Ice maker operation refers to the processes involved in producing ice within a refrigerator. This includes the operational logic, temperature checks, and fault modes that are critical for diagnosing and ensuring proper ice production.

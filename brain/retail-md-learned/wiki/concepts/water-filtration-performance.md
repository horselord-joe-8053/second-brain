# Water Filtration Performance

Water filtration performance refers to the effectiveness of the refrigerator's water filter in removing contaminants from drinking water. 

## Key Metrics

- **Rated Capacity**: 200 gallons (757 liters).
- **Contaminant Reduction**:
  - Chlorine taste/odor: 97.0% average reduction.
  - Lead: greater than 99.3% at pH 6.5.
  - Cyst: greater than 99.99%.

Understanding the filtration performance is essential for consumer awareness regarding water safety and the efficacy of the water filter.

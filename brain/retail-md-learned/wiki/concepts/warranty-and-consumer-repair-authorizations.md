# Warranty and Consumer Repair Authorizations

Warranty and consumer repair authorizations refer to the policies regarding what repairs are covered under warranty and who is authorized to perform them. This concept highlights the limitations of consumer repair capabilities and the need for professional assistance in appliance repairs.

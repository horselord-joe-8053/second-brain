# Smart Grid Demand Response

Smart Grid Demand Response is a feature that allows the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator to adjust its energy consumption based on signals from utility companies. This functionality is important for energy management and efficiency.

## Benefits
- **Energy Efficiency:** Reduces energy consumption during peak demand times.
- **Cost Savings:** Potentially lowers energy bills by optimizing usage.

# Electrical Safety

Electrical safety refers to the guidelines and precautions necessary for safely handling electrical components in appliances. It is a critical aspect emphasized in the *Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide*, which outlines the importance of preventing injury or damage during repairs. The guide is intended for trained technicians only, highlighting the risks associated with improper handling of electrical systems.

# Diagnostic Modes

Diagnostic modes are specific operational settings that technicians can access to diagnose issues within appliances, such as the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. These modes provide a structured approach to troubleshooting and identifying faults, as detailed in the *Samsung RF28K9070SG Repair Guide*. The guide outlines the steps for entering these modes and interpreting the results.

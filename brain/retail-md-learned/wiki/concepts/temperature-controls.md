# Temperature Controls

Temperature controls in the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator are electronically operated mechanisms that allow users to set and adjust the temperature in both the refrigerator and freezer compartments. Proper temperature settings are crucial for food safety and energy efficiency.

- **Recommended Settings:**
  - Refrigerator: 33°F to 45°F (0°C to 7°C)
  - Freezer: -5°F to 5°F (-21°C to -15°C)

Users can adjust the temperature settings by following the procedures outlined in the user guide, ensuring that food is stored at optimal temperatures.

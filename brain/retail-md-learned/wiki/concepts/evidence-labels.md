**Evidence Labels** are classifications used in user guides to indicate the reliability of the information presented. They help users assess the validity of instructions and claims made within the guide. 

The two primary labels are:
- **[DOCUMENTED]:** Information that is directly supported by the manual.
- **[UNKNOWN]:** Information that is not established in the reviewed source or remains uncertain.

This classification system is particularly useful in the troubleshooting context, distinguishing between documented and unknown information to ensure clarity in the reliability of instructions and guidance provided in the manual. 

In the context of the Samsung RF28K9070SG user manual, Evidence Labels serve to categorize information as `[DOCUMENTED]` or `[UNKNOWN]`, further aiding users in understanding the reliability of the information provided.

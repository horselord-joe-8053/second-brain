# Energy Management System (EMS)

The Energy Management System (EMS) refers to a system that monitors and manages energy consumption in smart appliances, including the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. It is designed to optimize energy efficiency and integrate with smart grid technology.

## Key Features
- Monitoring of energy usage
- Integration with smart grid systems
- Support for demand response programs

## Importance
The EMS is crucial for enhancing the energy efficiency of appliances and enabling users to participate in energy-saving initiatives through smart grid technology.

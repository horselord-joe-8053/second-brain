# Condenser Maintenance

Condenser maintenance is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator. 

- **Cleaning Recommendations:** Routine cleaning of the condenser is not necessary under normal conditions. However, in dusty or greasy environments, cleaning every 2-3 months is advised.

- **Cleaning Procedure:**
  1. Unplug the refrigerator.
  2. Remove the base grille.
  3. Vacuum accessible areas with a soft brush.
  4. Replace the grille and reconnect.

Following these guidelines helps ensure the refrigerator operates efficiently and effectively.

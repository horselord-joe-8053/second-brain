The Main Board, SMPS (Switched-Mode Power Supply), and Inverter Board are critical components of the Samsung RF28K9070 refrigerator. They manage power distribution and control various functions within the appliance. Understanding their layout and safety precautions is essential for effective repair and maintenance.

# Crisper Drawers

Crisper drawers in the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator are designed to store fruits and vegetables while regulating humidity levels to enhance freshness. 

- **Humidity Regulation:** The humidity in these drawers is automatically controlled, eliminating the need for user adjustments.

These drawers play a crucial role in maintaining the quality and longevity of produce stored in the refrigerator.

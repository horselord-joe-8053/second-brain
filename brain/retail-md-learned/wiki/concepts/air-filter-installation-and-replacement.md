# Air Filter Installation and Replacement

Air filter installation and replacement is an important maintenance task for the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator, aimed at reducing odors and maintaining freshness inside the refrigerator.

## Procedure

1. Identify the installed housing for the air filter.
2. Remove the used air filter by squeezing the side tabs.
3. Install the new air filter according to the specified orientation.

**Recommendation**: Replace the air filter approximately every six months to ensure optimal performance.

# Diagnostic Records

Diagnostic records are specific numerical records used to identify issues within the operation of the Samsung RF28K9070SG refrigerator. These records provide critical data for troubleshooting and ensuring proper functionality.

## Importance

- Essential for accurate diagnosis of appliance issues.
- Aids in maintaining optimal performance and reliability.

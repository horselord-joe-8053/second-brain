# Water Filtration System

The water filtration system in the KitchenAid KRFF507HPS refrigerator is designed to filter water for the dispenser and icemaker, ensuring safe drinking water and high-quality ice. This system includes installation, maintenance, and replacement instructions critical for the appliance's performance.

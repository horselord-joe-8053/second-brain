**Parts and Features** refer to the various components of the KitchenAid French Door Bottom Mount Refrigerator that contribute to its functionality. Understanding these parts is essential for proper usage and maintenance. Key components include:
- Ice maker
- Air filter
- Water filter
- Door bins
- Crispers
- Pantry drawer
- Freezer drawer
- LED ramp-on lighting
- In-Door-Ice® dispensing system
- Pull-out tray

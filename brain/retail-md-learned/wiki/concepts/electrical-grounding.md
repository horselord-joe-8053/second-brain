# Electrical Grounding

Electrical grounding is the practice of connecting electrical devices to the ground to prevent electrical shock. It is essential for the safe operation of refrigerators, as improper grounding can lead to hazards. GE refrigerators require a grounded three-prong outlet, and users are advised against removing the ground prong or using adapter plugs.

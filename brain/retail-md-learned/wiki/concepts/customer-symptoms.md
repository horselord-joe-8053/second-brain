Customer symptoms are descriptions of issues reported by users of appliances, such as refrigerators. These symptoms serve as the basis for troubleshooting actions and are crucial for effective user support. The Samsung RF28K9070SG troubleshooting guide includes various customer symptoms, such as the refrigerator not operating, food freezing, and unusual noises, each accompanied by specific checks and solutions to address the issues.

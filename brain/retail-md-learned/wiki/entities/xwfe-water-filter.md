# XWFE Water Filter

The XWFE Water Filter is designed for use in certain GE refrigerator models, including the GNE27JYMFS. It is responsible for filtering water and ice, ensuring that the water dispensed is clean and safe for consumption. The user guide includes installation instructions, maintenance tips, and troubleshooting information related to the XWFE filter.

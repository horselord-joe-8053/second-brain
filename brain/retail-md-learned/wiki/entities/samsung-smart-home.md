Samsung Smart Home is an application designed to control and monitor Samsung smart appliances, including the RF28K9070 refrigerator. The app allows users to manage settings, receive notifications, and troubleshoot issues remotely. It plays a crucial role in the functionality of smart features in Samsung refrigerators.

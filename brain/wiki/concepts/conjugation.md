# Conjugation

Conjugation is the variation of the form of a verb in accordance with tense, mood, voice, aspect, person, number, gender, and case. This concept is essential for language learners, particularly in Portuguese, where verb forms change significantly based on these factors. The document serves as a comprehensive guide for verb conjugation in Portuguese, detailing various moods and tenses.

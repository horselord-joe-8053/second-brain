Flávio Bolsonaro is a Senator from the Liberal Party (PL) and a current candidate in the 2026 Brazilian presidential election. He received the highest percentage of votes in the first round, making him a significant political figure and the son of former President Jair Bolsonaro. His candidacy represents a continuation of his father's political legacy.

His potential presidency is analyzed in the context of significant shifts in foreign policy and electoral strategy. He is likely to pivot Brazil towards U.S. alliances, potentially reshaping the country's foreign policy and its relationship with China.
